# St. Paul
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** June 29 (Feast of Saints Peter and Paul)
**Era:** 1st Century AD (~5 AD – ~67 AD)
**Origin:** Tarsus, Cilicia (modern Turkey)
**Patronage:** Missionaries, writers, journalists, theologians, Gentile Christians
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Paul of Tarsus started out as the most dangerous enemy the early Church had — a highly educated Pharisee who hunted Christians down to have them imprisoned or killed — until a blazing light knocked him off his horse on the road to Damascus and the Risen Jesus spoke to him personally. Completely transformed in a single blinding moment, he became the greatest missionary in history, sailing and walking over 10,000 miles across the Roman Empire to plant churches in city after city, and writing letters that became a massive portion of the New Testament. Paul's story is the ultimate proof that no one is too far gone for God, that a radical encounter with Jesus can overturn an entire life in an instant, and that your greatest weakness can become your greatest mission.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Gleaming sword — "the sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God" (Ephesians 6:17); also the instrument of his martyrdom by beheading in Rome under Nero
- Scroll of epistles — he authored 13 New Testament letters that have shaped Christian theology and spirituality for two thousand years
- Damascus blinding light — the blazing light of his conversion encounter with the Risen Christ on the Damascus road, the moment his entire life pivoted from persecution to proclamation
