# St. Paul
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** June 29
**Era:** 1st Century AD (died ~67 AD)
**Origin:** Tarsus, Cilicia (modern Turkey)
**Patronage:** Missionaries, writers, journalists, theologians, Gentile Christians
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Paul was a zealous Pharisee who actively hunted down and persecuted Christians — until a blinding light knocked him off his horse on the road to Damascus and Jesus spoke to him directly. Completely transformed, he became the greatest missionary in history, traveling 10,000 miles by sea and land to plant churches across the Roman Empire, and writing letters that became a huge portion of the New Testament. Paul's story is the ultimate proof that no one is too far gone for God, and that a radical encounter with Jesus can change everything in an instant.
