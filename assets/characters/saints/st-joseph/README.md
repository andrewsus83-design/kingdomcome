# St. Joseph
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** March 19 (Solemnity of St. Joseph) / May 1 (St. Joseph the Worker)
**Era:** 1st Century BC – 1st Century AD (~45 BC – ~18 AD)
**Origin:** Nazareth, Galilee (modern Israel)
**Patronage:** The Universal Church, workers, fathers, families, carpenters, a holy death
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joseph of Nazareth was a humble carpenter chosen by God for the most extraordinary job in the history of the universe: to be the earthly father of Jesus Christ and the protector of the Holy Family. He never speaks a single recorded word in all four Gospels — he simply acts, obeys, and loves without complaint, rising in the middle of the night when angels speak to him and leading Mary and the baby Jesus to safety through the dark. Joseph is the ultimate example of quiet, steady, faithful strength — a man who showed his love entirely through what he did, and who trusted God even when nothing made sense.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- White lily staff — his absolute purity of heart and his miraculous election by God above all other men for this sacred and hidden role
- Carpenter's tools (square and chisel) — his trade, his vocation, and the dignity of honest manual labor; Jesus of Nazareth learned these very skills under Joseph's patient hands
- Wood shavings and sawdust — his hiddenness and ordinariness; the greatest saint after Mary lived in a workshop and died unknown to the world, yet God entrusted him with the Son of God
