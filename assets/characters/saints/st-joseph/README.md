# St. Joseph
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** March 19 (Solemnity); May 1 (St. Joseph the Worker)
**Era:** 1st Century BC–1st Century AD
**Origin:** Nazareth, Galilee (modern Israel)
**Patronage:** The Universal Church, workers, fathers, families, a happy death, carpenters
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joseph was a humble carpenter from Nazareth chosen by God for the most important job in all of history: to be the earthly father of Jesus and the protector of the Holy Family. He never speaks a single word in the Gospels — he simply acts, obeys, and loves, getting up in the middle of the night when an angel speaks to him and leading his family to safety. For every young person, Joseph is the greatest model of what it means to be quietly strong, faithful, and trustworthy: a man who showed his love through what he did, not what he said.

- White lily staff — his purity and his special election by God, chosen above all other men for this sacred role
- Carpenter's tools — his trade, his vocation, and the dignity of honest labor; Jesus learned these skills at his hands
