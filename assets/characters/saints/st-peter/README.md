# St. Peter
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** June 29
**Era:** 1st Century AD (died ~64 AD)
**Origin:** Bethsaida, Galilee (modern Israel)
**Patronage:** The papacy, fishermen, net makers, Rome, longevity
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Peter was a rough fisherman from Galilee who became Jesus's closest companion and the first Pope of the Church. He was impulsive and sometimes made mistakes — he even denied Jesus three times — but he repented with tears, was forgiven, and went on to lead the early Church with great courage, dying for his faith in Rome. Peter is the saint for every young person who has ever fallen down and wondered if they can still be loved by God: his life answers with a thundering YES.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Two golden keys — the keys of the Kingdom of Heaven given by Christ: binding and loosing on earth and in heaven
- Fishing net — reminder of his origins as a fisherman and Christ's call to become a "fisher of men"
- Rock — Jesus named him "Petros" (Rock), the foundation on which the Church would be built
