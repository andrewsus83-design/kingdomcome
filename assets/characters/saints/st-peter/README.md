# St. Peter
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** June 29 (Feast of Saints Peter and Paul)
**Era:** 1st Century AD (~1 BC – ~64 AD)
**Origin:** Bethsaida, Galilee (modern Israel)
**Patronage:** Popes, fishermen, net makers, Rome, longevity, locksmiths
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Peter was a big, rough, sun-burned fisherman from the shores of Galilee when Jesus walked up to his boat one morning and changed his life forever. Impulsive and passionate, he was the first to confess Jesus as the Son of God — and also the one who denied knowing Him three times the night Jesus was arrested. But Jesus looked at him, forgave him, and said "Feed my sheep," and Peter went on to lead the early Church with lion-hearted courage, eventually dying upside-down on a cross in Rome because he felt unworthy to die the same way as his Lord. Peter is the saint for every young person who has ever messed up badly and wondered if God still loves them — his whole life is God's answer: yes, always, completely.

- Two golden crossed keys — the Keys of the Kingdom of Heaven given directly by Christ: "Whatever you bind on earth shall be bound in heaven" (Matthew 16:19); symbol of papal authority for 2,000 years

- Rooster — the rooster that crowed after his three denials; his emblem of repentance, tears, and the mercy that restored him
