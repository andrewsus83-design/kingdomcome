# Blessed Virgin Mary
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 15 (Assumption) / January 1 (Mary, Mother of God)
**Era:** 1st Century BC – 1st Century AD (~18 BC – ~45 AD)
**Origin:** Nazareth, Galilee (modern Israel)
**Patronage:** All Catholics, mothers, the universal Church, the human race
**Rarity:** Divine

## Description
Mary of Nazareth is the most honored of all the saints — the Mother of God Himself, chosen before the foundation of the world to bring Jesus Christ into history. She said "yes" to God's impossible plan as a young teenage girl in an ordinary village, and that single act of total trust and courage changed all of creation forever. Kids who discover Mary find the most powerful ally in heaven: a mother who walks with her children through every danger, intercedes before the throne of God without ceasing, and never, ever lets go of the ones she loves.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Crown of 12 stars — drawn from Revelation 12:1, the Woman clothed with the sun; her coronation as Queen of all Heaven and Earth, reigning beside her Son
- Crescent moon beneath her feet — symbol of her complete victory over darkness and sin, and her Immaculate Conception; she stands above all earthly and fallen things
- Red roses and white lilies at her feet — deep crimson roses represent her role as the Mystical Rose and her sorrow at the Cross; white lilies her perfect purity and the Immaculate Conception
