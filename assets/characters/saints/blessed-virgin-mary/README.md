# Blessed Virgin Mary
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 15 (Assumption); January 1 (Mary, Mother of God); many others throughout the year
**Era:** 1st Century BC–1st Century AD
**Origin:** Nazareth, Galilee (modern Israel)
**Patronage:** All of humanity, the Church, mothers, the sick, students, and every nation on earth
**Rarity:** DIVINE

## Description
Mary of Nazareth is the mother of Jesus Christ and the greatest human being who has ever lived. A teenage girl in an obscure village was asked by the Angel Gabriel to become the Mother of God, and she said yes with complete trust: "Let it be done to me according to your word." She stood at the foot of the Cross when everyone else fled, was assumed body and soul into heaven, and has appeared around the world for centuries guiding her children home. She is the Queen of all the saints, and the most powerful intercessor in the Kingdom.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Twelve-star crown — from Revelation 12, the Woman clothed with the sun; her royal dignity as Queen of Heaven
- Blue mantle — represents heaven, truth, and her role as the great protector who covers her children
- Crescent moon — she stands on the moon, above all earthly things, symbol of her Immaculate Conception and purity
