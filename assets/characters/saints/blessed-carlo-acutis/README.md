# Blessed Carlo Acutis
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** October 12
**Era:** 20th–21st Century (1991–2006)
**Origin:** Milan, Italy
**Patronage:** Internet users, computer programmers, young people, youth ministry
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Carlo Acutis was a completely normal Italian teenager from Milan who loved soccer, video games, Pokémon, his cat Pol, and his dog Briciola — and who also happened to be so deeply in love with Jesus in the Eucharist that he received Communion every single day, spent hours in Eucharistic adoration, and used his self-taught computer programming skills to build the world's first website cataloguing Eucharistic miracles from around the globe. He was beatified in 2020 and canonized in 2025, making him the first millennial saint — a young person who showed the world that holiness is not a museum exhibit from the past but the most radical, exciting, and available choice of any generation, including right now. Carlo called the Eucharist his "highway to heaven" and offered his leukemia for the Pope and the Church when he died at fifteen. Every kid who loves technology, gaming, or the internet has a patron who gets it completely.
