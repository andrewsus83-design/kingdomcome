# Blessed Carlo Acutis
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** October 12
**Era:** 21st Century (1991–2006)
**Origin:** Milan, Italy
**Patronage:** Internet users, computer programmers, young people
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Carlo Acutis was a completely normal Italian teenager who loved soccer, video games, and Pokémon — and also happened to be deeply in love with Jesus in the Eucharist. He used his computer programming skills to build the world's first website cataloguing Eucharistic miracles around the globe. He died of leukemia at just 15, offering his suffering for the Pope and the Church. Carlo proves that holiness is available to every young person, right now, using exactly the gifts they already have.
