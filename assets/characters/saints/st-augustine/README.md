# St. Augustine of Hippo
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 28
**Era:** 4th–5th Century (354–430 AD)
**Origin:** Thagaste, Numidia (modern Algeria)
**Patronage:** Brewers, printers, theologians, sore eyes, scholars
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Augustine spent his youth chasing pleasure and fame, living far from God while his mother Monica wept and prayed for him. After years of searching in philosophy and false religions, he heard a child's voice say "take up and read" — opened Paul's letter to the Romans — and was converted on the spot. He became Bishop of Hippo and one of the greatest thinkers in history, writing the Confessions, an autobiography of his soul's journey home to God. His story is for every young person who feels the restless ache inside: Augustine found the answer is always Jesus.
