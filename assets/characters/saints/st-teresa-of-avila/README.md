# St. Teresa of Ávila
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** October 15
**Era:** 16th Century (1515–1582)
**Origin:** Ávila, Castile, Spain
**Patronage:** Spain, chess players, headache sufferers, people in need of grace
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Teresa of Ávila was a sharp-witted, witty, and spiritually brilliant Carmelite nun who reformed her entire religious order and wrote some of the greatest masterworks of Christian mysticism ever produced. Her book "The Interior Castle" describes the soul as a beautiful castle with many rooms, with God dwelling at the very center, waiting to be discovered. She was so deep in prayer that she literally levitated — and her writings remain a guide for millions who want to go deeper with God. Teresa shows young people that prayer is an adventure into a vast interior world of wonder.
