# St. Patrick
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** March 17
**Era:** 5th Century (385–461 AD)
**Origin:** Roman Britain (modern Wales/Scotland), missionary to Ireland
**Patronage:** Ireland, Nigeria, engineers, snakes excluded from Ireland
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Patrick was a Romano-British teenager who was kidnapped by Irish pirates and enslaved for six years, spending his captivity in prayer on cold hillsides with sheep. After escaping, he heard God calling him back to Ireland — the very land that had enslaved him — to share the faith. He converted nearly all of Ireland, famously using the three-leafed shamrock to explain the Trinity. His story of forgiving his captors and returning in love is one of the most powerful examples of courage and mercy in history.
