# St. Joan of Arc
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** May 30
**Era:** 15th Century (1412–1431)
**Origin:** Domrémy, France
**Patronage:** France, soldiers, prisoners, people ridiculed for their faith
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joan of Arc was a teenage farm girl from rural France who heard the voices of saints calling her to lead the French army to victory during the Hundred Years' War. At just 17 years old she donned armor and changed the course of history, saving her nation against impossible odds. She is the ultimate example for young people that God calls ordinary kids to do extraordinary things — and that courage means doing what is right even when the whole world says you can't.
