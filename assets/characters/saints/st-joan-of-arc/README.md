# St. Joan of Arc
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** May 30
**Era:** 15th Century (1412–1431)
**Origin:** Domrémy, Lorraine, France
**Patronage:** France, soldiers, prisoners, people ridiculed for their faith, captives
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joan of Arc was a peasant farm girl from rural France who began hearing the voices of St. Michael, St. Catherine, and St. Margaret at age thirteen, telling her that God had chosen her to drive the English out of France and crown the rightful king. At just seventeen she walked into the court of the uncrowned French prince, convinced him she was sent by God, donned a suit of white armor, and led the French army to a string of miraculous victories that changed the course of European history. Captured, tried by corrupt churchmen loyal to her enemies, and burned at the stake at nineteen, she forgave her executioners and died with the name of Jesus on her lips — and was declared a saint five hundred years later. Joan is the proof that God picks the most unlikely people for the most impossible missions.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Full silver plate armor — she wore real armor in real battle, given to her by the French army; it represents faith made visible through radical obedience to God's call
- White banner with fleur-de-lis — the standard she carried into every battle rather than a weapon; she said she loved her banner forty times more than her sword because it bore the name of God
- Cross-hilted longsword — she famously retrieved an ancient sword buried behind an altar in the church of Sainte-Catherine-de-Fierbois, fulfilling a prophecy; the cross-shaped hilt symbolizes fighting only in Christ's name
