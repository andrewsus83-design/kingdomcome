# St. Ignatius of Loyola
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** July 31
**Era:** 16th Century (1491–1556)
**Origin:** Loyola, Basque Country, Spain
**Patronage:** Jesuits, soldiers, spiritual directors, educators, spiritual retreats
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Ignatius of Loyola was a vain, ambitious, hot-tempered Spanish knight obsessed with military glory and impressing ladies at court — until a French cannonball shattered his leg at the Battle of Pamplona in 1521, and he spent months in a castle bed with nothing to read but lives of the saints. Those months of forced stillness gave him something no battlefield ever had: time to listen to God. The Spiritual Exercises he wrote in that recovery became the most influential book of practical spirituality in history, used by millions every year, and the Society of Jesus he founded sent missionaries to every continent and transformed education worldwide. For young people who want to know what God actually wants them to do with their one life, Ignatius is the master of discernment — the art of telling the difference between what your heart truly wants and what God is calling you toward.
