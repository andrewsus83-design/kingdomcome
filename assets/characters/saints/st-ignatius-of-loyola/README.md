# St. Ignatius of Loyola
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** July 31
**Era:** 16th Century (1491–1556)
**Origin:** Loyola, Basque Country, Spain
**Patronage:** Jesuits, soldiers, educators, retreats, spiritual directors
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Ignatius of Loyola was a vain, ambitious Spanish soldier who was badly wounded in battle and spent months in bed with nothing to read but lives of the saints. Those books changed everything. He invented the Spiritual Exercises — a powerful system of prayer and discernment still used by millions today — and founded the Jesuits, the most influential teaching and missionary order in history. For young people, Ignatius is the master of figuring out what God wants you to do with your life and then going after it with everything you have.
