# St. Thérèse of Lisieux
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** October 1
**Era:** 19th Century (1873–1897)
**Origin:** Alençon, France
**Patronage:** Missions, France, florists, aviators, those suffering from illness
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Thérèse of Lisieux entered the Carmelite convent at age 15 and died at just 24, never doing anything famous by the world's standards — yet she became one of the most beloved saints in history. She discovered the "Little Way," her teaching that ordinary everyday acts of love, done with great devotion, are just as holy as grand heroic deeds. For every young person who feels small or ordinary, Thérèse shows that the tiniest act of kindness offered to God can change the world.
