# St. Thomas Aquinas
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** January 28
**Era:** 13th Century (1225–1274)
**Origin:** Aquino, Kingdom of Sicily (modern Italy)
**Patronage:** Students, scholars, universities, theologians, Catholic schools, pencil makers
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Thomas Aquinas was born into a noble family who wanted him to be a powerful abbot, but he scandalously ran away to join the poor Dominican friars and was kidnapped by his own brothers to stop him — and he still became a Dominican. The greatest theologian and philosopher in the history of the Church, his masterwork the Summa Theologica runs to over 3,500 pages and answers nearly every question ever asked about God, the soul, ethics, and the universe with razor-sharp clarity. His fellow students at university called him "the Dumb Ox" because he was big, quiet, and said little — but his teacher St. Albert the Great told them: "That ox's bellowing will one day fill the world." Kids who love asking hard questions, who love knowing why things are true rather than just what they're told, will find in Thomas their greatest champion.
