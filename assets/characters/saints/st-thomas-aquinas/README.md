# St. Thomas Aquinas
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** January 28
**Era:** 13th Century (1225–1274)
**Origin:** Aquino, Italy
**Patronage:** Students, scholars, universities, theologians, Catholic schools
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Thomas Aquinas was one of the greatest thinkers in all of human history — a Dominican friar who showed that faith and reason work together, not against each other. His masterwork, the Summa Theologica, answered the hardest questions about God, morality, and the universe with brilliant clarity. Kids who love learning and asking "why?" will find in Thomas a champion who proves that being smart and being holy are a perfect team.

- **Virtue:** Wisdom
