# St. Maria Goretti
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** July 6
**Era:** 20th Century (1890–1902)
**Origin:** Corinaldo, Italy
**Patronage:** Youth, girls, teenage girls, victims of assault, purity, poverty
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Maria Goretti was an 11-year-old Italian farm girl so poor she had never attended school, but so filled with faith that her courage became legendary across the world. When she died forgiving her attacker — and when that attacker later converted and attended her canonization — the world saw the most powerful demonstration of Christian forgiveness imaginable. Maria is the saint for every young person who has ever been hurt, or who struggles to forgive someone who hurt them, showing that God's grace can do the impossible inside a human heart.
