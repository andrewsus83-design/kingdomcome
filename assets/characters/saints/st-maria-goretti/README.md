# St. Maria Goretti
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** July 6
**Era:** 20th Century (1890–1902)
**Origin:** Corinaldo, Marche, Italy
**Patronage:** Youth, girls, teenage girls, victims of assault, purity, forgiveness, poverty
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Maria Goretti was an eleven-year-old Italian farm girl so poor she had never attended a single day of school, living with her family in the Pontine Marshes south of Rome in a crumbling building shared with another poor family. When Alessandro Serenelli, the nineteen-year-old son of the family, attacked her and she refused to give in, he stabbed her fourteen times. She died the following day in the hospital — but before she died, she told the priest she forgave Alessandro completely and wanted him to be with her in heaven. Alessandro spent twenty-seven years in prison, was visited in a dream by Maria offering him white flowers, converted entirely, and eventually stood next to Maria's elderly mother at her canonization in 1950. Maria is the patron of every young person who has ever been hurt by someone who should have protected them, and the proof that the most impossible forgiveness is the most powerful gift you can give.
