# St. Dominic
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 8
**Era:** 13th Century (1170–1221)
**Origin:** Caleruega, Spain
**Patronage:** Astronomers, falsely accused people, the Dominican Order, the Rosary
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Dominic Guzmán founded the Order of Preachers — the Dominicans — to fight heresy not with swords but with truth, learning, and prayer. He was famous for staying up all night praying and weeping for souls, and tradition credits him with receiving the Rosary from the Blessed Virgin Mary. For young people, Dominic shows that prayer is the most powerful weapon in the universe, and that speaking the truth with love can change hearts more than anything else.
