# St. Dominic
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 8
**Era:** 12th–13th Century (1170–1221)
**Origin:** Caleruega, Castile, Spain
**Patronage:** Astronomers, falsely accused people, the Dominican Order, the Rosary
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Dominic Guzmán was a Spanish canon who encountered the Albigensian heresy — a false teaching that was destroying souls and tearing apart communities in southern France — and decided that the best way to fight false ideas was not with armies but with truth, learning, and prayer. He founded the Order of Preachers — the Dominicans — who were trained to preach with intellectual excellence and live in radical poverty, so no one could accuse them of preaching for money or comfort. He was legendary for staying up all night alone in prayer, weeping out loud for souls who were lost, and tradition credits Mary herself with giving him the Rosary as a spiritual weapon. Dominic shows every young person that the most powerful thing you can do when faced with a lie is become so well-grounded in truth that the lie has nowhere to stand.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Glowing rosary beads — Dominican tradition holds that Mary appeared to Dominic and gave him the Rosary as the most powerful weapon against heresy; the Dominicans are its primary promoters
- White lily — purity of heart and his deep Marian devotion; he is always depicted with Mary's flower as a sign of his consecration to her
- Golden star above forehead — before his birth his mother dreamed of a dog leaping from her womb carrying a torch that set the world on fire; Dominic's star is the prophetic sign of the light he brought
