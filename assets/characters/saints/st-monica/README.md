# St. Monica
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 27
**Era:** 4th Century (~331–387 AD)
**Origin:** Thagaste, Numidia (modern Algeria, North Africa)
**Patronage:** Mothers, wives, abuse victims, alcoholics, difficult marriages, converts
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Monica was a devout North African Christian woman who spent over seventeen years praying without ceasing for her brilliant, restless, morally chaotic son Augustine, who rejected Christianity, joined a cult, fathered a child out of wedlock, and moved across the Mediterranean to escape her. She followed him all the way to Milan, praying every single night, weeping so much for his soul that a bishop told her: "It is not possible that the son of so many tears should perish." He was right. Augustine converted and became one of the greatest theologians and saints in the history of the Church, and his most famous words — "Our heart is restless until it rests in You" — are the fruit of his mother's relentless love. Monica is the proof that no prayer is ever wasted and that patient faithful love is the most powerful force in the universe after God Himself.
