# St. Monica
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 27
**Era:** 4th Century (331–387 AD)
**Origin:** Thagaste, Numidia (modern Algeria)
**Patronage:** Mothers, wives, abuse victims, alcoholics, difficult marriages, converts
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Monica prayed for her son Augustine every single day for over 17 years while he lived a wild and sinful life far from God. Her tears and prayers never stopped, and eventually Augustine converted and became one of the greatest saints in history. Monica is the patron of every parent, every person who prays for someone they love who seems lost, and every young person who has been shaped by the faithful love of a praying mother. She proves that persistent prayer is never wasted.
