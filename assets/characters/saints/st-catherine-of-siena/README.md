# St. Catherine of Siena
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** April 29
**Era:** 14th Century (1347–1380)
**Origin:** Siena, Italy
**Patronage:** Italy, Europe, nurses, fire prevention, people ridiculed for their piety
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Catherine of Siena was the 24th child in her family, a girl who had no formal education but became so wise in God that popes and kings asked for her advice. She was a Doctor of the Church who dictated brilliant letters and her mystical work "The Dialogue" while in ecstasy. She personally persuaded Pope Gregory XI to return the papacy from France to Rome, saving the Church during a crisis. Catherine shows every young person that holiness and wisdom are not about your age, your grades, or where you come from.
