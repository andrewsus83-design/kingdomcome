# St. Clare of Assisi
**Category:** Saints

**Patronage:** Television, eye disease, needleworkers, good weather, laundry workers, goldsmiths
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Clare of Assisi was the most beautiful and sought-after noblewoman in Assisi — wealthy, educated, and admired — who secretly slipped out of her family's palazzo at midnight on Palm Sunday to follow Francis's call to radical poverty and prayer, and her family never forgave her. She founded the Poor Clares, a community of women who owned absolutely nothing individually or communally and lived entirely on what God provided through begging, becoming the first women's religious order in history to write their own rule of life. The most legendary moment of her life came when a Saracen army sent by the Emperor Frederick II surrounded Assisi and began scaling the walls of her convent — Clare rose from her sickbed, carried the golden monstrance containing the Blessed Sacrament to the window, and knelt before it in prayer. The soldiers fled in terror. Clare shows every young person that the power of the Eucharist is the greatest force on earth.

## Character Attributes (Symbols)
- Golden monstrance blazing with light — she used the Blessed Sacrament to repel the Emperor's army; she is the patron of Eucharistic adoration and one of its most dramatic biblical defenders
- White linen veil — her vow of consecrated virginity and simplicity; Francis himself cut her noble hair and replaced it with the poor veil on the night she fled to him
- Plain rope cord belt — total poverty; the Poor Clares owned nothing, not even their own habits, representing absolute trust that God alone is enough
