# St. Clare of Assisi
**Category:** Saints
**Feast Day:** August 11
**Era:** 13th Century (1194–1253)
**Origin:** Assisi, Italy
**Patronage:** Television, eye disease, embroiderers, laundry workers, good weather
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Clare of Assisi was a beautiful noble girl who ran away from home at night to follow St. Francis and live in radical poverty. She founded the Poor Clares — an order of nuns who owned nothing and lived entirely on what God provided — and governed it for 40 years. Legend says she once stopped an enemy army by kneeling before the Blessed Sacrament, and God's light drove them away. Clare shows young people that true beauty and strength come from within, and that trusting God completely is the bravest thing anyone can do.
