# JACOB
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 25–50; Hosea 12
**Era:** Patriarchal Period (~1850 BC)
**Role:** Patriarch — Father of the Twelve Tribes
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Jacob, whose name God changed to "Israel," was the grandson of Abraham and father of the twelve sons who became the Twelve Tribes of Israel. He had a famous dream of a ladder reaching from earth to heaven with angels ascending and descending, and he boldly wrestled with an angel of God all night until he received a blessing. Jacob's determination and his deep faith in God's promises shaped the destiny of an entire nation.
