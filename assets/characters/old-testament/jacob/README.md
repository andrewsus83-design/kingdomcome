# JACOB
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 25–50; Hosea 12; Romans 9; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Patriarchal Era (~1850 BC)
**Role:** Father of the Twelve Tribes — Third Patriarch of Israel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Jacob was the grandson of Abraham, and God changed his name to "Israel" — meaning "he who wrestles with God" — after he spent an entire night wrestling with a divine being and refused to let go until he received a blessing, which is about the most heroic thing anyone could ever do. He had a famous dream of a magnificent ladder stretching from earth to heaven with angels ascending and descending on it, showing that earth and heaven are connected through God's people. Jacob fathered twelve sons who became the Twelve Tribes of Israel, making him the founding father of an entire nation through whom the Savior of the world would one day come.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Perseverance
- **Special Ability:** Jacob's Ladder — Jacob raises his staff and a blazing golden ladder of divine light erupts from the ground into the heavens, opening a portal through which twelve warrior angels descend to defend the kingdom for 40 seconds, each bearing the symbol of one of the twelve tribes on their shield.
- **Passive Bonus:** Israel's Blessing — All twelve unit slots in the kingdom receive a +10% stats boost simultaneously while Jacob is active, representing the twelve tribes united under their father's covenant blessing.
- **Unlock Cost:** 1200 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 3
