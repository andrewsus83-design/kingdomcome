# JOSEPH (SON OF JACOB)
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 37–50; Acts 7; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Egyptian Sojourn (~1700 BC)
**Role:** Dreamer and Savior — Viceroy of Egypt
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joseph was Jacob's beloved son who was given a spectacular coat of many colors by his father — and then sold into slavery in Egypt by his own jealous brothers, thrown into prison for a crime he didn't commit, and forgotten by the people he helped. But God had an extraordinary plan: Joseph rose from slave to prisoner to the second most powerful man in all of Egypt because of his God-given ability to interpret dreams, and he ultimately saved millions of people — including his own family — from a catastrophic seven-year famine. His jaw-dropping story of betrayal transformed into forgiveness and redemption is one of the most powerful pictures of Jesus in the entire Bible, the one who was rejected by his own people and yet became the savior of the world.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Forgiveness
- **Special Ability:** Coat of Many Colors — Joseph opens his magnificent coat wide and a spectacular rainbow shimmer of light radiates outward across the entire map, instantly granting the kingdom complete immunity to all resource shortages, famine events, and economic attacks for 60 seconds; every building glows with a multi-colored prosperity aura.
- **Passive Bonus:** Interpreter of Dreams — At the start of each new game day, Joseph reveals one hidden enemy strategy, incoming threat, or bonus resource cache through a golden dream vision, giving players advance warning and preparation time.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 4
