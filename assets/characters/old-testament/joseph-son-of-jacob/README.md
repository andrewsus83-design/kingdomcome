# JOSEPH (SON OF JACOB)
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 37–50; Acts 7
**Era:** Patriarchal Period / Egyptian Sojourn (~1750 BC)
**Role:** Patriarch — Dreamer and Viceroy of Egypt
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Joseph was Jacob's beloved son who was given a spectacular coat of many colors by his father. His jealous brothers sold him into slavery in Egypt, but God turned this terrible act into something wonderful — Joseph rose from slave to the second most powerful man in Egypt, where he saved millions of people from famine. Joseph's story of forgiveness and trust in God's plan is one of the most beautiful in the entire Bible.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Forgiveness
- **Special Ability:** Coat of Many Colors — Grants your kingdom immunity from resource shortages and famine events for 60 seconds, rainbow shimmer effect
- **Unlock Cost:** 3500 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 3
