# EVE
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 1–5; Romans 5; 2 Corinthians 11; 1 Timothy 2
**Era:** Era of Creation (~4000 BC)
**Role:** Mother of All Living
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Eve is the first woman and the mother of all humanity — created by God himself from Adam's side as his perfect partner and companion, showing that women stand equal beside men in dignity, purpose, and glory. She was the first to cry out in wonder at the miracle of new life, declaring "I have gotten a man with the help of the Lord!" at the birth of her son Cain. Though her story includes the tragedy of the Fall in the Garden, God promised that one of her descendants — Jesus, born of a woman — would crush the head of evil and restore everything that was lost, making Eve's line the most important family tree in history.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Life-Giving Love
- **Special Ability:** Promise of the Seed — Eve raises her hand and a blazing golden star streaks across the sky, calling down a radiant beam of divine light that purifies all corrupted tiles on the map, removes all enemy debuffs from allied units, and stuns every enemy for 8 seconds; golden particles drift down for the duration as a reminder of the first Messianic promise.
- **Passive Bonus:** Mother of Nations — All allied hero units in the kingdom gain +15% maximum health as a permanent bonus while Eve is present, reflecting her role as the source of all human life.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
