# EVE
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 2–4
**Era:** Creation (Beginning of Time)
**Role:** Matriarch — First Woman
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Eve was the first woman, lovingly created by God from Adam's rib as his companion and partner. She was given the great gift of being the mother of all living, and her name means "life-giver." Though she was deceived by the serpent in the Garden, God promised that a woman's offspring would one day crush the serpent — a beautiful prophecy pointing toward Mary and Jesus.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Life-Giving Love
- **Special Ability:** Mother of All Living — Doubles the growth rate of all buildings in your kingdom for 60 seconds
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
