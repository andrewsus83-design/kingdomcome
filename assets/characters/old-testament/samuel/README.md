# SAMUEL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Samuel 1–28; Acts 3; 13; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Late Period of the Judges (~1060 BC)
**Role:** Last Judge and First Kingmaker — Prophet and Priest
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Samuel was dedicated to God before he was even born by his faithful and determined mother Hannah, and as a young boy sleeping in the Tabernacle he heard God calling his name three times in the night — one of the most beloved and tender stories in all of Scripture. He became the last and greatest Judge of Israel and its first major prophet since Moses, speaking God's word to the nation with absolute authority, anointing both Saul and David as kings with holy oil, and standing as a moral anchor for Israel during some of its most turbulent years. Samuel's extraordinary life — from miraculous birth to lifelong service — shows that God calls people of every age to do mighty things for His kingdom, and that listening to God's voice is the beginning of all true leadership.
