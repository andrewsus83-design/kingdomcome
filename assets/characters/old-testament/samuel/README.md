# SAMUEL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Samuel 1–25; Acts 3; 13
**Era:** Period of the United Monarchy (~1050 BC)
**Role:** Prophet / Judge — Last Judge, First Major Prophet
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Samuel was dedicated to God before he was even born by his faithful mother Hannah, and as a young boy sleeping in the Tabernacle he heard God calling his name — one of the most beloved stories in the Bible. He became the last great Judge of Israel and the first major prophet, anointing both Saul and David as kings with holy oil. Samuel's life shows that God can call anyone, even a child, to do mighty things for His kingdom.
