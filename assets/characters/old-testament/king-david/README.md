# KING DAVID
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Samuel 16–31; 2 Samuel 1–24; 1 Kings 1–2; Psalms; Acts 2; 13; Matthew 1
**Era:** United Kingdom of Israel (~1000 BC)
**Role:** Warrior King and Psalmist — Man After God's Own Heart
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
David was the greatest king in Israel's history — a young shepherd boy from Bethlehem who trusted God enough to run toward the giant Goliath with only a sling and five smooth stones, became the greatest military commander of his age, and wrote the beautiful Psalms that believers have prayed for three thousand years. God made an eternal covenant with David, promising that his throne and his descendant would reign forever — a promise fulfilled completely and perfectly in Jesus Christ, who is called "Son of David" throughout the New Testament. Despite making serious and devastating mistakes, David's extraordinary capacity for deep repentance and passionate love for God show us that what matters most to God is not perfection but a heart that always comes back to Him.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Repentance and Praise
- **Special Ability:** Song of David — David raises his golden harp and plays a thunderous heavenly melody; golden musical notes and light waves cascade across the entire battlefield, instantly restoring all allied units to full health, stunning all enemies for 25 seconds with wonder, and triggering a golden sunrise visual that fills the entire screen as the music plays.
- **Passive Bonus:** Shepherd King's Heart — David's presence grants the kingdom a permanent +20% HolyPoints generation rate, reflecting the river of praise that flowed from his heart his entire life; additionally, all shepherd and pastoral buildings produce resources 25% faster.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
