# KING DAVID
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Samuel 16–31; 2 Samuel; 1 Kings 1–2; Psalms; Acts 2; 13
**Era:** United Kingdom of Israel (~1010–970 BC)
**Role:** King / Prophet / Psalmist — Shepherd King and Man After God's Own Heart
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
David was the greatest king of Israel and is called "a man after God's own heart" — a young shepherd boy who killed the giant Goliath with a single stone from his sling, became a mighty king, and wrote many of the beautiful Psalms in the Bible. God made an eternal covenant with David, promising that his descendant would reign forever — a promise fulfilled perfectly in Jesus Christ, who is called "Son of David." Despite making serious mistakes, David's deep repentance and love for God show us how to come back to God no matter what.

## Game Stats
