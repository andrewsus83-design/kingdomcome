# JOSHUA
**Testament:** Old Testament

**Era:** Conquest of Canaan (~1240 BC)
**Role:** Military Leader / Judge — Conqueror of the Promised Land
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Joshua was Moses' courageous successor who led the Israelites across the Jordan River into the Promised Land after 40 years of wandering in the desert. He was famous for the miraculous Battle of Jericho — where God caused the city walls to crumble simply by the Israelites marching around them and blowing their trumpets. Joshua's name in Hebrew is the same root as "Jesus," meaning "God saves," and his conquest of Canaan is a powerful symbol of Christ's victory over sin.
