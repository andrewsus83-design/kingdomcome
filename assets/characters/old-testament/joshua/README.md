# JOSHUA
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus 17; Numbers 13–14; Joshua 1–24; Acts 7; Hebrews 4
**Era:** Conquest of Canaan (~1250 BC)
**Role:** Military Commander — Conqueror of the Promised Land
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joshua was Moses' fearless successor — the only one of his generation bold enough to trust God's promise when he and Caleb alone gave a positive report after scouting the Promised Land, declaring "We can certainly do it!" while ten others cowered in fear. After forty years of desert wandering he finally led the Israelites across the Jordan River into Canaan, and God gave him one of the most spectacular battlefield victories in history at Jericho — the city's massive walls collapsed simply because God's people marched around them and blew their trumpets in faith. Joshua's very name is the Hebrew form of "Jesus," meaning "God saves," making his conquest of the Promised Land one of the clearest pictures of Christ's ultimate victory over sin and death in the entire Old Testament.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Courageous Faith
- **Special Ability:** Walls of Jericho — Joshua raises the ram's horn and seven mighty blasts echo across the battlefield; a shockwave of divine golden energy radiates outward from him, instantly destroying all enemy fortifications, defensive walls, and shield barriers on the map while allied units pour through the breach with a +50% attack boost for 10 seconds.
- **Passive Bonus:** Strong and Courageous — All military units in the kingdom have +15% attack and +15% defense while Joshua is present, inspired by God's repeated command to him: "Be strong and courageous!" (Joshua 1:9).
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 4
