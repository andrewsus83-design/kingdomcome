# JEREMIAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Jeremiah; Lamentations
**Era:** Late Kingdom of Judah / Babylonian Conquest (~627–580 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — The Weeping Prophet
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Jeremiah is known as the "Weeping Prophet" because he loved his people so deeply that he wept over their sins and the terrible destruction of Jerusalem. He was called by God as a young man — probably a teenager — and told God he was too young to speak, but God touched his lips and gave him His words. Jeremiah foretold a "New Covenant" written not on stone tablets but on human hearts, a beautiful prophecy fulfilled by Jesus at the Last Supper.
