# JEREMIAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Jeremiah 1–52; Lamentations; Matthew 2; 16
**Era:** Late Kingdom of Judah / Babylonian Conquest (~627–586 BC)
**Role:** Weeping Prophet — Herald of the New Covenant
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Jeremiah is known as the "Weeping Prophet" — not because he was weak, but because he loved his people so deeply and God's heart so completely that he couldn't stop grieving over the sins that were leading Israel to destruction. He was called by God as a young teenager, tried to protest that he was too young to speak, but God touched his lips and said "I have put my words in your mouth." For over 40 years Jeremiah faithfully declared God's word while being mocked, imprisoned, thrown into a cistern, and rejected by nearly everyone — and he never gave up. Most remarkably, he prophesied a "New Covenant" not written on stone tablets but engraved on human hearts — a stunning prediction fulfilled by Jesus at the Last Supper and written by the Holy Spirit in every believer's heart today.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Compassionate Perseverance
- **Special Ability:** New Covenant — Jeremiah holds his scroll to his heart and golden light blazes outward in a pulsing heartbeat pattern; all character wounds are instantly healed, one recently destroyed building is immediately restored, and all allied units receive the "Heart Covenant" buff granting +30% resistance to all attacks for 20 seconds — because God's word written on the heart can never be taken away.
- **Passive Bonus:** Like Fire in His Bones — The prophetic fire Jeremiah described (Jeremiah 20:9) inspires the kingdom; allied units can never be silenced or have their abilities suppressed by enemy actions while Jeremiah is present, and all prophetic character abilities cost 20% less HolyPoints to activate.
