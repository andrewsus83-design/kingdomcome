# DANIEL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Daniel; Matthew 24; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Babylonian and Persian Exile (~605–535 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — Lion's Den Hero and Interpreter of Dreams
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Daniel was a young Jewish teenager taken captive to Babylon who refused to eat the king's food or bow to false gods — and God rewarded his faithfulness with extraordinary wisdom and the ability to interpret dreams. He survived being thrown into a den of hungry lions because an angel shut the lions' mouths, proving that God protects those who trust Him. Daniel's visions of "one like a Son of Man" coming on the clouds of heaven are among the most direct prophecies of Jesus in the entire Old Testament.
