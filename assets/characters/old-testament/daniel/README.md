# DANIEL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Daniel 1–12; Matthew 24; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Babylonian and Persian Exile (~605–530 BC)
**Role:** Prophet in Exile — Interpreter of Dreams and Visions
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Daniel was a young Jewish teenager — possibly as young as 14 — when he was taken captive to Babylon, and he immediately showed breathtaking courage by refusing to eat the king's food and defile himself, trusting God to keep him healthy on vegetables alone. God rewarded his integrity with extraordinary wisdom and the supernatural ability to interpret dreams, and Daniel rose to become one of the most powerful men in the most powerful empire on earth. When he refused to stop praying to God despite a royal decree, he was thrown into a den of starving lions — and emerged the next morning completely unharmed, because God sent an angel to shut the lions' mouths. Daniel's visions of "one like a Son of Man" coming on the clouds of heaven are among the most direct and detailed prophecies of Jesus in the entire Old Testament.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Uncompromising Integrity
- **Special Ability:** Den of Lions — Daniel kneels in prayer and a radiant golden angel with enormous wings materializes around him, extending a blazing dome of protection across all allied units; for 30 seconds no allied unit can be harmed by any attack, while the lions surrounding them sit peacefully with mouths firmly closed, their golden eyes glowing with divine calm.
- **Passive Bonus:** Integrity Never Compromises — While Daniel is in the kingdom, no allied unit can be bribed, corrupted, turned against its allies, or have its abilities suppressed by enemy powers; Daniel's absolute refusal to compromise is contagious, and the entire kingdom is 25% more resistant to all morale-breaking attacks.

- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 4
