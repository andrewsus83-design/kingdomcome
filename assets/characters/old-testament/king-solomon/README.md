# KING SOLOMON
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Kings 1–11; 2 Chronicles 1–9; Proverbs; Ecclesiastes; Song of Solomon; Matthew 6; 12
**Era:** United Kingdom of Israel (~970–930 BC)
**Role:** King — Wisest King of Israel, Builder of the Temple
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
King Solomon was the son of David and the wisest person who ever lived — when God offered him anything he wanted, Solomon asked not for wealth or power, but for wisdom to govern God's people well. God was so pleased that He gave Solomon both wisdom and incredible wealth. Solomon's greatest achievement was building the magnificent Temple in Jerusalem, the most beautiful building in the ancient world, as a permanent home for God among His people.
