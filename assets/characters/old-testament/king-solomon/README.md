# KING SOLOMON
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Kings 1–11; 2 Chronicles 1–9; Proverbs; Ecclesiastes; Song of Solomon; Matthew 6; 12
**Era:** United Kingdom of Israel (~970 BC)
**Role:** Wisest King — Builder of the Temple
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
King Solomon was David's son and the wisest person who ever lived — when God appeared to him at night and offered him anything he wished for, Solomon asked not for wealth, fame, or the lives of his enemies, but for wisdom to govern God's people well, and God was so pleased that He gave Solomon wisdom beyond measure AND great wealth and honor as a bonus. Solomon's greatest achievement was building the breathtaking Temple in Jerusalem — the most magnificent building in the ancient world, constructed with cedar from Lebanon, gold from Ophir, and the finest craftsmen of the age — as a permanent dwelling place for God among His people. Though Solomon ultimately stumbled in his later years, his writings in Proverbs, Ecclesiastes, and the Song of Solomon have guided believers in wisdom and love for three thousand years.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** God-Given Wisdom
- **Special Ability:** Wisdom of Solomon — Solomon holds up his golden scepter and a brilliant shaft of divine wisdom-light illuminates the entire kingdom; for 30 seconds the optimal strategy for every active challenge is revealed, all puzzle events are instantly solved, hidden bonuses in each building are activated, and all enemy weaknesses are exposed to all allied units.
- **Passive Bonus:** Temple Builder — All building construction costs are reduced by 20% and all cathedral and monastery upgrades produce 30% more HolyPoints while Solomon is active, inspired by his legendary achievement of building God's most glorious earthly dwelling.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
