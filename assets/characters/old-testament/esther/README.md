# ESTHER
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Esther
**Era:** Persian Empire (~480 BC)
**Role:** Queen — Brave Jewish Queen of Persia

## Description
Esther was a young Jewish woman who became Queen of Persia and risked her life by going before the king uninvited — an action punishable by death — to save her entire people from a wicked plot to destroy them. Her cousin Mordecai encouraged her with the famous words, "Who knows whether you have not come to the kingdom for such a time as this?" Esther's courage and faith show that God places each of us in exactly the right place and time to do great things for His people.
