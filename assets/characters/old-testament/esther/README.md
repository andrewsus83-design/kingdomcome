# ESTHER
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Esther 1–10
**Era:** Persian Empire (~480 BC)
**Role:** Brave Queen — Savior of the Jewish People
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Esther was a young Jewish orphan raised by her cousin Mordecai who became Queen of the Persian Empire through a remarkable series of God's providential moves — and then faced the most terrifying choice of her life when the wicked official Haman convinced the king to issue an edict to exterminate every Jewish person in the entire empire. Mordecai challenged her with words that ring through history: "Who knows whether you have not come to the kingdom for such a time as this?" — and Esther chose courage over safety, saying "If I perish, I perish," and going to the king unbidden in a move that could have meant her death. Her courage, wisdom, and faith saved her entire people, and the feast of Purim is celebrated by Jewish communities every year to this day in memory of Esther's heroism.
