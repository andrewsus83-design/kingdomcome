# JOB
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Job 1–42; James 5; Ezekiel 14
**Era:** Patriarchal Period (Era Unknown — possibly ~2000 BC)
**Role:** Man of Patience — Righteous Sufferer
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Job was the most righteous and prosperous man of his age — deeply faithful to God, generous to the poor, and renowned for his integrity — and then in a single terrible day he lost everything: his children, his flocks, his wealth, and his health, all while his friends insisted it must be his own fault. Job refused to curse God or give up his faith even when he couldn't understand why God had allowed such suffering, and he asked God the hardest questions any human heart can ask — and God answered him from a whirlwind, showing him the breathtaking complexity and glory of creation as the only answer big enough for suffering. In the end God restored everything Job had lost and gave him twice as much as before, and Job's story stands as the great biblical answer to every person who has ever asked "Why does God let good people suffer?" — the answer being that God is big enough to hold our questions, faithful enough to answer us, and powerful enough to restore everything.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Patient Endurance
- **Special Ability:** Restored Double — After any major defeat or significant resource loss, Job kneels in prayer and a divine whirlwind of golden light spirals upward from his position; all resources lost in the last 60 seconds are restored at double the original amount, and all defeated allied units during the same period return at full health — because with God, faithful suffering is never the final word.
- **Passive Bonus:** Friends in the Ashes — Job's presence in the kingdom makes every ally more resilient; all units can withstand 25% more damage before being defeated, and units that are defeated take 30% longer to be eliminated permanently, reflecting Job's extraordinary endurance that outlasted every attack.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 2
