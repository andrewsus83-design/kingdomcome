## Description
Job was a righteous and wealthy man who lost everything — his children, his health, and his possessions — and yet refused to curse God or give up his faith even when he didn't understand why he was suffering. He asked God the hardest questions and God answered him from a whirlwind, showing him the wonders of creation. In the end God restored everything Job had lost and gave him twice as much as before, teaching us that faithful suffering is never the last word.
