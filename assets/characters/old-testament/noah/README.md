# NOAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 6–9; Hebrews 11; 1 Peter 3; Matthew 24
**Era:** Pre-Flood Era (~2400 BC)
**Role:** Builder of the Ark — Covenant Keeper
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Noah was the one righteous man in an entire world full of corruption, and God chose him for the most impossible-sounding mission in history — build a colossal wooden ark to save his family and two of every kind of animal from a worldwide flood. For 100 years Noah hammered, sawed, and built while the world around him laughed, but when the rains came, the ark sailed and Noah's stubborn faith preserved the future of all life on earth. After the flood God sealed an everlasting covenant with Noah, placing a magnificent rainbow in the sky as a promise that echoes all the way forward to the covenant God makes with us through Jesus.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Faithfulness
- **Special Ability:** Ark of Salvation — Noah plants his staff in the ground and a colossal golden ark materializes above the battlefield, sending out an expanding dome of protective white-and-gold light that covers all allied units, making them completely invulnerable for 12 seconds; a brilliant rainbow arcs across the sky behind the dome, signaling God's covenant protection.
- **Passive Bonus:** Righteous Builder — All construction and upgrade times for kingdom buildings are reduced by 25% while Noah is present, reflecting his legendary skill as builder of the greatest ship in the ancient world.
- **Unlock Cost:** 4000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 4
