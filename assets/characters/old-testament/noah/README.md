# NOAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 6–9
**Era:** Antediluvian Period (Before the Flood)
**Role:** Patriarch — Ark Builder
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Noah was a righteous man chosen by God to build a massive wooden ark and save his family and two of every animal from a great flood. He trusted God completely even when everyone around him laughed at him for building a boat on dry land. After the flood, God made a covenant with Noah and set a beautiful rainbow in the sky as a sign of His promise to never again destroy the earth by water.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Faithfulness
- **Special Ability:** Rainbow Covenant — Shields your entire kingdom from all enemy attacks for 45 seconds, rainbow arc visual effect
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 4
