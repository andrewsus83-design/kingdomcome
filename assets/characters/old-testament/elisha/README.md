# ELISHA
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Kings 19; 2 Kings 2–13
**Era:** Divided Kingdom (~850 BC)
**Role:** Miracle Prophet — Successor of Elijah
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Elisha was Elijah's devoted disciple who asked boldly for a double portion of his mentor's prophetic spirit — and received it, going on to perform more recorded miracles than any other prophet in the entire Old Testament. He healed a deadly pot of stew with flour, multiplied a widow's small jar of oil until she had enough to sell and pay all her debts, raised a Shunammite boy from the dead, healed the Syrian general Naaman of leprosy by having him wash seven times in the Jordan River, and made an iron ax head float on water. Elisha's ministry of healing, abundance, and restoration points beautifully forward to the miracles of Jesus, who also healed the sick, raised the dead, and multiplied food for the hungry.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Generous Compassion
- **Special Ability:** Double Portion — Elisha raises his staff and his inherited mantle blazes with divine light; all resource production in the kingdom — coins, HolyPoints, food, wood, and stone — is instantly doubled for 50 seconds while golden overflow effects pour from every building, inspired by the widow's oil that never ran out until every vessel was filled.
- **Passive Bonus:** Prophet's Healing Touch — Every 60 seconds Elisha automatically heals the most wounded allied unit in the kingdom back to full health, and any unit standing near him regenerates +8% health per second, reflecting his extraordinary ministry of healing and restoration.
- **Unlock Cost:** 1200 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 3
