# ELISHA
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Kings 19; 2 Kings 2–13
**Era:** Divided Kingdom (~850–800 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — Miracle Worker and Successor of Elijah
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Elisha was Elijah's devoted disciple who asked for and received a double portion of his mentor's prophetic spirit. He went on to perform more recorded miracles than any other prophet in the Old Testament — multiplying oil and bread, healing a deadly stew, raising a boy from the dead, and curing the Syrian general Naaman of leprosy by telling him to wash in the Jordan River seven times. Elisha's ministry of healing and abundance points beautifully toward the miracles of Jesus.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Generosity
- **Special Ability:** Double Portion — Doubles all resource production (coins, HolyPoints, building materials) for 50 seconds
- **Unlock Cost:** 3500 HolyPoints
