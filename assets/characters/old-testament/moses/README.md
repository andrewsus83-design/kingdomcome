# MOSES
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers; Deuteronomy; Acts 7; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Egyptian Exodus (~1300 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — Lawgiver and Liberator
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Moses is one of the greatest figures in the entire Bible — chosen by God at a burning bush in the desert to lead the Israelites out of 400 years of brutal slavery in Egypt, through the parted Red Sea on dry ground, and all the way to the edge of the Promised Land. He spoke face-to-face with God on Mount Sinai and received the Ten Commandments written by God's own finger on stone tablets, then guided God's people through 40 years in the wilderness, performing mighty miracles with his divine staff along the way. Moses is the supreme prophet and lawgiver of the Old Testament, and the New Testament calls him a foreshadow of Jesus — the ultimate Liberator who leads his people not out of Egypt but out of sin and death into eternal life.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Courage
- **Special Ability:** Parting the Waters — Moses raises his staff with both hands and a colossal golden wave visual sweeps the entire screen, instantly clearing all obstacles, enemy barriers, and fortifications from the battlefield; allied units gain a path directly to their objectives and receive a +40% speed boost for 12 seconds.
- **Passive Bonus:** Lawgiver's Authority — All allied units in the kingdom fight with +20% attack strength while Moses is present, inspired by the divine authority he carried from his encounters with God on Sinai.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
