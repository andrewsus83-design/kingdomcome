# MOSES
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers; Deuteronomy; Acts 7; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Egyptian Exodus (~1300 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — Lawgiver and Liberator
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Moses is one of the greatest figures in the entire Bible — chosen by God to lead the Israelites out of slavery in Egypt and through the Red Sea on dry ground. He spoke face-to-face with God in the burning bush and received the Ten Commandments on Mount Sinai. Moses spent 40 years guiding God's people through the desert, performing mighty miracles with his staff, and pointing forward to Jesus as the ultimate liberator.
