# ABRAHAM
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 11–25; Romans 4; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Patriarchal Period (~2000 BC)
**Role:** Patriarch — Father of Faith
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Abraham is called the "Father of Faith" because he trusted God so completely that he left his homeland without knowing where he was going, and was willing to offer his only son Isaac in sacrifice. God made an everlasting covenant with Abraham, promising that his descendants would be as numerous as the stars and that through him all nations of the earth would be blessed. He is the spiritual father of Christianity, Judaism, and Islam.
