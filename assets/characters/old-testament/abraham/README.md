# ABRAHAM
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 12–25; Romans 4; Galatians 3; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Patriarchal Era (~2000 BC)
**Role:** Father of Faith — Father of Many Nations
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Abraham left everything he knew — his homeland, his family, his comfort, and his security — simply because God asked him to, and that one act of total trust changed the entire history of the world forever. God made an extraordinary covenant with this one man: that his descendants would be as countless as the stars in the night sky, that all nations of the earth would be blessed through him, and that one day his family would inherit a promised land flowing with milk and honey. Abraham is called "the father of faith" because he believed God's seemingly impossible promises without wavering, and Christians, Jews, the whole story of salvation, and Jesus himself trace their roots straight through his extraordinary life.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Faith
- **Special Ability:** Stars of Promise — Abraham raises his staff toward the night sky and a cascade of brilliant golden stars rains down across the entire map, each star landing on an allied unit and granting it a full health restoration and a +25% attack and defense boost for 15 seconds; the sky blazes with uncountable constellations for the entire duration.
- **Passive Bonus:** Father of Nations — Every time a new allied unit is recruited into the kingdom, it arrives with a +20% bonus to maximum health, reflecting Abraham's legacy as the founding patriarch of a great and blessed people.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
