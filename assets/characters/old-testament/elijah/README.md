# ELIJAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Kings 17–21; 2 Kings 1–2; Malachi 4; Matthew 11; 17; Luke 1
**Era:** Divided Kingdom (~870–850 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — Greatest Prophet of the Northern Kingdom
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Elijah was one of the mightiest prophets who ever lived — he called down fire from heaven, raised the dead, and was taken up to heaven in a fiery chariot without ever dying. He boldly challenged 450 false prophets of Baal in a dramatic contest on Mount Carmel and proved that the Lord alone is God. Elijah appears with Moses at the Transfiguration of Jesus, representing the Prophets alongside the Law, and was foretold to return before the Messiah comes.
