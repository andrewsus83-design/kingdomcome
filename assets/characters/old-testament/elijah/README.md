# ELIJAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Kings 17–21; 2 Kings 1–2; Malachi 4; Matthew 11; 17; Luke 1; James 5
**Era:** Divided Kingdom (~870 BC)
**Role:** Prophet of Fire — Champion of the Lord
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Elijah was one of the mightiest prophets who ever lived — a wild, fearless man of God who called down fire from heaven, shut the rain for three years with a single prayer, raised a dead boy back to life, and outran a chariot for miles in God's strength. He boldly walked alone into the court of the wicked King Ahab and challenged 450 false prophets of Baal to a dramatic fire-calling contest on Mount Carmel, and when his prayer called down divine fire that consumed the altar, the water, and the stones themselves, all of Israel fell on their faces crying "The Lord, He is God!" Elijah is so important that he never died — God sent a fiery chariot and took him directly to heaven in a whirlwind — and he appears with Moses at the Transfiguration of Jesus, representing all the Prophets standing with the Messiah.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Zeal for God
- **Special Ability:** Fire from Heaven — Elijah thrusts his arm skyward with a cry of holy boldness and a colossal column of divine golden-orange fire blazes down from heaven, consuming every enemy unit on the screen in a spectacular flash of glory and leaving their positions scorched with golden light; the entire sky turns orange and gold for 8 seconds as afterglow.
- **Passive Bonus:** Still Small Voice — When Elijah is in the kingdom, all allied units cannot be frightened, demoralized, or routed by enemy actions; they always fight with steady courage, inspired by the still small voice that strengthened Elijah even in his most exhausted moment at the broom tree (1 Kings 19:12).
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
