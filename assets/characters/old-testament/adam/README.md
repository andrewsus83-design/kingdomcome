# ADAM
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 1–5; Romans 5; 1 Corinthians 15
**Era:** Era of Creation (~4000 BC)
**Role:** Father of Humanity
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Adam is the first man ever created — formed by God himself from the dust of the ground and breathed into life by God's own breath, making him utterly unique among all living creatures. He walked with God in the Garden of Eden, named every animal on earth, and was given the sacred mission of caring for all of creation as God's steward. Though his disobedience brought suffering into the world, his story points forward to Jesus — called the "Last Adam" — who came to undo the Fall and restore humanity to the friendship with God that Adam once knew.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Stewardship
- **Special Ability:** Breath of Life — Adam raises both hands skyward and a brilliant golden wind sweeps across the entire kingdom, instantly restoring all allied buildings to full health and waking sleeping allies with glowing particles of divine breath; the effect lasts 10 seconds and grants every unit a +30% vitality boost for the duration.
- **Passive Bonus:** Garden Keeper — All resource-generating buildings in the kingdom produce 20% more food, wood, and stone while Adam is active, reflecting his original mandate to tend and cultivate creation.
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
