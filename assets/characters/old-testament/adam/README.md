# ADAM
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 1–5
**Era:** Creation (Beginning of Time)
**Role:** Patriarch — First Man
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Adam was the very first human being, created by God from the dust of the earth and given the breath of life. He lived in the beautiful Garden of Eden, named all the animals, and was given the special mission of caring for God's creation. Though he made the first great mistake by disobeying God, his story teaches us about God's infinite love and plan for humanity's redemption.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Stewardship
- **Special Ability:** Breath of Life — Revives all defeated allies in your kingdom with 50% health restored
- **Unlock Cost:** 5000 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 5
