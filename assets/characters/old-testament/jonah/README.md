# JONAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Jonah 1–4; Matthew 12; 16; Luke 11
**Era:** Divided Kingdom / Assyrian Period (~760 BC)
**Role:** Reluctant Prophet — Sign of the Messiah
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Jonah was a prophet who tried to run away from God's call by booking a sea voyage in exactly the opposite direction from where God was sending him — only to discover, in the most dramatic way possible, that you can never outrun God's love and purpose for your life. He was swallowed by a great fish and spent three days in complete darkness inside its belly, praying until he finally surrendered — and Jesus himself pointed to this as the greatest sign of his own death and resurrection: "Just as Jonah was three days in the belly of the great fish, so the Son of Man will be three days in the heart of the earth." When Jonah finally preached in the great city of Nineveh, over 120,000 people repented in the greatest mass conversion in the Old Testament, proving that God's mercy and love extend far beyond the people who think they deserve it.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Surrender and Mercy
- **Special Ability:** Sign of Jonah — Jonah raises his hands and a colossal great fish leaps out of the water in the background, its glowing belly visible; a golden wave of divine mercy rolls across the entire map, instantly converting all neutral characters into allies, causing 30% of enemy units to abandon their attack and retreat in repentance, and granting the kingdom a +500 bonus HolyPoints for 40 seconds.
- **Passive Bonus:** Second Chances — Jonah's presence means no ally is ever truly out — any allied unit that has been defeated in this game session has a 25% chance to return spontaneously with half health, reflecting the theme of God's inexhaustible willingness to give second chances to anyone who calls on Him.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 2
