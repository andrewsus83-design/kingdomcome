# JONAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Jonah; Matthew 12; 16; Luke 11
**Era:** Divided Kingdom / Assyrian Period (~780–750 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — The Reluctant Prophet
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Jonah was a prophet who tried to run away from God's call, only to find that you can never outrun God's love and plan for your life. He was swallowed by a great fish and spent three days in its belly — just as Jesus would spend three days in the tomb — before being spit out onto shore and finally completing his mission. When Jonah preached in the great city of Nineveh, over 120,000 people repented, showing that God's mercy extends to all people, even our enemies.
