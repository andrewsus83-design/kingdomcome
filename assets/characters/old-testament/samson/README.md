# SAMSON
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Judges 13–16; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Period of the Judges (~1100 BC)
**Role:** Nazirite Judge — Champion of Israel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Samson was one of the most physically powerful men who ever lived — a Nazirite consecrated to God from before birth, whose extraordinary supernatural strength came not from muscle but from his unbroken covenant with God, signified by his long uncut hair. He killed a lion with his bare hands, defeated 1,000 Philistine warriors single-handedly using only the jawbone of a donkey, and carried the gates of an entire city up a hillside on his shoulders. Though he stumbled badly and was betrayed by Delilah and captured, his final act of repentant faith — praying to God and bringing down the Philistine temple with his bare hands — killed more enemies in one moment than in all his previous battles combined, showing that God's grace is more powerful than all our failures.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** God-Given Strength
- **Special Ability:** Pillars of Strength — Samson plants his feet and seizes two massive stone pillars; divine golden energy blazes through his entire body as he tears them apart, sending a colossal shockwave across the battlefield that instantly defeats the two strongest enemy units and stuns every enemy within range for 20 seconds in a spectacular display of supernatural power.
- **Passive Bonus:** Nazirite's Consecration — While Samson is in the kingdom, all allied military units gain a +25% physical attack bonus; if Samson is ever defeated in battle, this bonus increases to +50% as allies fight in his honor.
- **Unlock Cost:** 1200 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 3
