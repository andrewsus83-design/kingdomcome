# SAMSON
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Judges 13–16
**Era:** Period of the Judges (~1100 BC)
**Role:** Judge — Mighty Champion of Israel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Samson was one of the most powerful men who ever lived, chosen by God before his birth to be a Nazirite set apart for His service. His extraordinary strength — which came from God through his uncut hair — allowed him to defeat a lion with his bare hands and fight off thousands of enemies single-handedly. Though he struggled with temptation and weakness, his final act of faith in pulling down the pillars of his enemies' temple showed that God's power could still work through a repentant heart.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Strength (through God)
- **Special Ability:** Pillars of Strength — Destroys the two strongest enemy units on the field and stuns all nearby enemies for 20 seconds
- **Unlock Cost:** 3500 HolyPoints
