# SARAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 11–23; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Patriarchal Period (~2000 BC)
**Role:** Matriarch — Mother of Nations
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Sarah was Abraham's beloved wife and the mother of Isaac, born miraculously when she was 90 years old — proof that nothing is impossible for God. She laughed when God told her she would have a child in her old age, and her son Isaac's name means "laughter" as a reminder of God's joyful surprise. Sarah is honored as the mother of the Israelite nation and a woman of deep faith.
