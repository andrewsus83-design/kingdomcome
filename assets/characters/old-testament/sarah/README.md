# SARAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 11–23; Romans 4; Hebrews 11; 1 Peter 3
**Era:** Patriarchal Era (~2000 BC)
**Role:** Mother of Nations — First Matriarch of Israel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Sarah was Abraham's beloved wife and the first great matriarch of Israel — a woman of remarkable beauty, deep faith, and extraordinary courage who left her homeland to follow God's call alongside her husband. When three angels appeared and told the elderly Sarah she would have a son within a year, she laughed in disbelief — she was 90 years old! — but God kept His promise and her son Isaac was born, and his very name means "laughter" as a perpetual reminder of God's delightful surprises. Sarah is honored throughout Scripture as a woman of strong faith and noble character, and the Apostle Peter holds her up as a model for all women of courage and trust in God.
