**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Tobit was a faithful Jewish man living in exile in Nineveh who remained devoted to God's law, gave generously to the poor, and bravely buried the bodies of his fellow Israelites at great personal risk. When he became blind and fell into deep poverty, he never lost faith in God. God sent the Archangel Raphael in disguise to accompany Tobit's son Tobias on a journey, where Raphael helped defeat a demon, find a wife, and obtain the medicine to heal Tobit's blindness.
