# TOBIT
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Tobit 1–14 (Deuterocanonical)
**Era:** Assyrian Exile (~700 BC)
**Role:** Man of Prayer and Charity — Model of Faithful Family Life
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Tobit was a faithful Jewish man living in exile in Nineveh who kept every detail of God's law even when it was dangerous and costly — he gave generously to the poor, provided clothing to the naked, and bravely buried the bodies of his fellow Israelites even when the Assyrian king had forbidden it and threatened to kill him for it. When he became blind through a freak accident and fell into desperate poverty, Tobit never lost faith or stopped praying, even when he begged God to let him die. God heard his prayer and sent the Archangel Raphael in disguise as a traveling companion to guide Tobit's son Tobias on a long journey — and through Raphael's guidance, the demon tormenting Tobias's future wife was defeated, a marriage was made, and the fish-gall medicine that cured Tobit's blindness was obtained, showing that God's providence works in every ordinary moment of faithful family life.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Faithful Charity
- **Special Ability:** Angel's Guidance — Tobit folds his hands in prayer and the Archangel Raphael materializes in golden radiant armor beside the most vulnerable allied unit in the kingdom; for 35 seconds that unit is completely immune to all damage and moves at double speed, guided by an angelic escort; at the ability's end, Raphael delivers a healing touch that fully restores any wounds taken.
- **Passive Bonus:** Almsgiving Shield — Tobit's faithful life of charity creates a protective spiritual atmosphere; every time the player spends HolyPoints on upgrades or buildings, the kingdom receives a 10% bonus resource return, reflecting the biblical principle that generous giving always returns multiplied to the giver.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 2
