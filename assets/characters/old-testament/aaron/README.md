# AARON
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers
**Era:** Egyptian Exodus (~1300 BC)
**Role:** High Priest — First Aaronic Priest
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Aaron was Moses' older brother and the first High Priest of Israel, appointed by God to lead all worship and sacrifice for the Israelite people. He wore spectacular priestly vestments — a breastplate with twelve jewels representing the twelve tribes — and carried a golden censer burning holy incense before God. When he budded a dead staff overnight with almonds, God confirmed Aaron's divine calling as High Priest.
