# AARON
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus; Leviticus; Numbers; Hebrews 5–10
**Era:** Egyptian Exodus (~1300 BC)
**Role:** First High Priest of Israel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Aaron was Moses' older brother, his spokesperson before Pharaoh, and the first High Priest of Israel — appointed by God himself to lead all worship and sacrifice for the entire Israelite nation, making him one of the most important religious figures in all of history. He wore spectacular priestly vestments including a jeweled breastplate with twelve precious stones representing the twelve tribes of Israel, and he burned holy incense before God twice each day in the Tabernacle. When his wooden staff miraculously budded, blossomed, and bore almonds overnight, God used it to confirm Aaron's divine calling as High Priest, and his office is called the forerunner of Jesus, the eternal High Priest who intercedes for us before God forever.

- **Passive Bonus:** Priestly Intercession — While Aaron is in the kingdom, all resource costs for worship-related buildings and upgrades are reduced by 20%, and the Cathedral generates bonus HolyPoints every 60 seconds.
- **Unlock Cost:** 1200 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 3
