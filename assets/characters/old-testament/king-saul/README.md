# KING SAUL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Samuel 9–31
**Era:** United Kingdom of Israel (~1050–1010 BC)
**Role:** King — First King of Israel
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Saul was the very first King of Israel — a tall, handsome young man chosen by God and anointed by the prophet Samuel. He started his reign with great humility and won important battles for Israel, but pride and disobedience eventually led him to lose God's blessing. Saul's story is a powerful reminder that true leadership requires humility and obedience to God above all else, and that God looks at the heart more than outward appearances.
