# KING SAUL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** 1 Samuel 9–31; Acts 13
**Era:** United Kingdom of Israel (~1020 BC)
**Role:** First King of Israel
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Saul was the very first King of Israel — a tall, strikingly handsome young man from the tribe of Benjamin who started his reign with great humility, actually hiding among the baggage when Samuel tried to present him to the people as their new king. He won important early battles for Israel and had every advantage a king could want, but pride, jealousy, and repeated disobedience to God's commands gradually cost him God's blessing and his kingdom. Saul's story is one of the Bible's most powerful cautionary tales — a reminder that God looks not at outward appearances, position, or talent, but at the heart, and that true greatness always begins and ends with obedience to God.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Humility (what he lost — a cautionary tale)
- **Special Ability:** First Among Equals — Saul raises his spear and golden light envelops the entire army; for 30 seconds the weakest allied unit on the field receives a full upgrade, matching the stats of the strongest unit, reminding players that God can raise up the humble and that no one in his kingdom should be considered unimportant.
- **Passive Bonus:** Royal Presence — While Saul is in the kingdom, all military units receive a +10% morale bonus and enemy units suffer a -10% intimidation penalty simply from facing Israel's first anointed king; however, if Saul is defeated, the kingdom morale drops by -15% as a warning about the cost of pride.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 3

- Bronze spear planted as a staff — symbol of his military prowess and also the jealous rage he showed when he hurled it at David
- Army encamped below on the plains — symbol of the mighty kingdom he was given and gradually lost through disobedience
