# MIRIAM
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus 2; 15; Numbers 12; 20; Micah 6:4
**Era:** Egyptian Exodus (~1300 BC)
**Role:** Prophetess and Worship Leader
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Miriam was a bold prophetess and the courageous older sister of Moses and Aaron who showed extraordinary bravery as a young girl — she watched over baby Moses as he floated in a basket in the crocodile-filled Nile River and cleverly arranged for their own mother to be hired as his nurse. After God parted the Red Sea and the Israelites crossed on dry ground, Miriam led all the women of Israel in one of the most spectacular celebrations in the Bible — singing, dancing, and playing tambourines in explosive joy on the far shore. God himself calls her alongside Moses and Aaron as one of the three great leaders he sent to guide his people out of Egypt, making her one of the most important women in the entire Old Testament.

- **Virtue:** Joyful Courage

- **Passive Bonus:** Prophetess's Watch — Miriam's presence in the kingdom acts like a watchtower; enemy surprise attacks are always detected 15 seconds early and the player receives a warning with a visual alert, reflecting her childhood role as Moses' guardian watchwoman.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 2
