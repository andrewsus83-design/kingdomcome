# MIRIAM
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Exodus 2; 15; Numbers 12; 20; Micah 6
**Era:** Egyptian Exodus (~1300 BC)
**Role:** Prophetess — Sister of Moses and Aaron
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Miriam was a bold prophetess and the courageous older sister of Moses and Aaron who watched over baby Moses as he floated in the Nile River in a basket. After the miraculous crossing of the Red Sea, she led all the women of Israel in joyful dancing and singing with tambourines — one of the first worship celebrations recorded in the Bible. She is honored as one of the great leaders God sent to guide His people out of Egypt.
