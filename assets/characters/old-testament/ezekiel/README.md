# EZEKIEL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Ezekiel
**Era:** Babylonian Exile (~593–570 BC)
**Role:** Prophet — Vision Prophet
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Ezekiel was a priest-prophet who lived among the Jewish exiles in Babylon and received the most spectacular visions of any prophet in the Bible. He saw the famous vision of the "wheel within a wheel" with four living creatures, which has captivated people for thousands of years. He also prophesied over the Valley of Dry Bones, where God breathed life into a great army of dead bones — a powerful symbol of resurrection and God's ability to bring hope from hopelessness.
