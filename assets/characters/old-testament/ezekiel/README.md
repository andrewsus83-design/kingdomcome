# EZEKIEL
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Ezekiel 1–48
**Era:** Babylonian Exile (~593–571 BC)
**Role:** Prophet of Visions — Priest-Prophet in Exile
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Ezekiel was a priest-prophet who was carried into exile in Babylon and then received the most spectacular and mind-bending visions of any prophet in the entire Bible, including the famous "wheel within a wheel" filled with the four living creatures — a vision so extraordinary that it has captivated, inspired, and puzzled readers for thousands of years. He acted out his prophecies in dramatic ways — lying on his side for over a year, drawing the siege of Jerusalem on a clay brick, and shaving his head — to get his message across to his fellow exiles. Most powerfully, Ezekiel prophesied over the Valley of Dry Bones, and as he spoke God's words, the bones rattled and came together, flesh covered them, and the Spirit of God breathed life into a vast army — one of the most stunning images of resurrection and hope in all of Scripture.
