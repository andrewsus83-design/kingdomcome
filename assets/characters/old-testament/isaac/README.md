# ISAAC
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Genesis 21–35; Romans 9; Galatians 4; Hebrews 11
**Era:** Patriarchal Era (~1900 BC)
**Role:** Son of Promise — Second Patriarch of Israel
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Isaac was the miracle son born to Abraham and Sarah in their extreme old age — his very existence was proof that God keeps His promises no matter how impossible they seem — and his name means "laughter," a permanent reminder of his mother's stunned joy. He is especially remembered for the most powerful story of trust in the entire Old Testament: when his father Abraham brought him to Mount Moriah to offer him as a sacrifice, Isaac walked faithfully up the mountain and submitted, and at the very last moment God provided a ram in his place — an event that powerfully foreshadows Jesus, the true Lamb of God, on the very same hill. Isaac later married Rebekah, dug wells of life-giving water across the dry land, and faithfully passed the covenant blessing to the next generation.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Obedient Trust
- **Special Ability:** The Ram Provided — Isaac kneels in prayer and a radiant golden ram appears on the battlefield, redirecting the three most powerful incoming attacks back at the enemies who launched them; golden light flashes and a divine horn sounds as each attack is deflected, symbolizing God's perfect provision.
- **Passive Bonus:** Wells of Life — Isaac's presence in the kingdom automatically generates a slow steady trickle of bonus water resources, and all allied units regenerate 5% health every 10 seconds, inspired by his gift for finding wells in dry places.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 2
