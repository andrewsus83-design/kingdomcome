# ISAAC
**Testament:** Old Testament

**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Isaac was the miracle son born to Abraham and Sarah in their old age, and his very name means "laughter." He is remembered for his extraordinary obedience when his father Abraham prepared to offer him as a sacrifice — and for his trust in God who provided a ram in his place. Isaac later became a loving husband to Rebekah, dug wells of water in the desert, and passed on God's covenant blessing to his sons Jacob and Esau.
