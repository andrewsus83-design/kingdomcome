# DEBORAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Judges 4–5
**Era:** Period of the Judges (~1200 BC)
**Role:** Prophetess and Judge — Military Commander of Israel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Deborah was the only female Judge of Israel and one of the most remarkable leaders in the entire Old Testament — simultaneously a respected prophetess who heard directly from God, a wise judge who settled the disputes of an entire nation, and a fearless military commander who led Israel to victory against an enemy with 900 iron war chariots. When the military general Barak refused to go into battle without her, she agreed but prophesied that the victory would go to a woman — and it did. The Song of Deborah that she composed after the victory is one of the oldest poems in all of Scripture and celebrates God's power working through unexpected people.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Divine Wisdom
- **Special Ability:** Deborah's Verdict — Deborah stands beneath her palm tree and pronounces God's judgment; a golden gavel of divine light slams down on the battlefield, instantly winning any active combat encounter, routing all fleeing enemies, and granting the player a +500 bonus HolyPoints for the righteous victory; golden light floods the scene for 8 seconds.
- **Passive Bonus:** Seat of Judgment — While Deborah is present, all disputes between allied units are resolved peacefully; allied units cannot turn against each other, and all kingdom morale bonuses are increased by 30%, reflecting her legendary wisdom as Israel's greatest judge.
- **Unlock Cost:** 1200 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Cathedral Level 3
