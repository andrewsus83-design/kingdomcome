# DEBORAH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Judges 4–5
**Era:** Period of the Judges (~1200 BC)
**Role:** Judge / Prophetess — Leader and Military Commander
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Deborah was the only female Judge of Israel and one of the greatest leaders in the entire Old Testament. She was both a wise judge who settled disputes under her famous palm tree and a mighty prophetess who declared God's word to the nation. She courageously led Israel's army to victory against the powerful General Sisera, and her victory song — the Song of Deborah — is one of the oldest poems in the Bible.
