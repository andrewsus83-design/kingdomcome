# RUTH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Ruth; Matthew 1 (genealogy)
**Era:** Period of the Judges (~1100 BC)
**Role:** Faithful Daughter-in-Law / Ancestor of King David
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Ruth was a young woman from Moab who chose to leave her homeland to stay with her mother-in-law Naomi after both their husbands died, speaking the beautiful words: "Where you go I will go, and where you stay I will stay; your people shall be my people and your God shall be my God." Her extraordinary loyalty and hard work gleaning leftover grain in the fields of Boaz led to a beautiful love story — and she became the great-grandmother of King David and an ancestor of Jesus.
