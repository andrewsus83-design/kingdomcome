# RUTH
**Testament:** Old Testament
**Book(s):** Ruth 1–4; Matthew 1:5 (genealogy of Jesus)
**Era:** Period of the Judges (~1100 BC)
**Role:** Loyal Foreigner — Ancestor of King David and Jesus
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Ruth was a young Moabite woman who, after losing her husband, chose to leave her homeland, her family, and everything she knew in order to stay with her grieving mother-in-law Naomi, speaking one of the most beautiful declarations of loyalty in all of Scripture: "Where you go I will go, and where you lodge I will lodge; your people shall be my people, and your God shall be my God." Her extraordinary faithfulness and humble hard work gleaning leftover grain in the fields of a man named Boaz led to a breathtaking love story — Boaz became her husband and redeemer, and Ruth became the great-grandmother of King David and a direct ancestor of Jesus himself, proving that God's family has always been open to anyone who chooses faithfulness and love.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Steadfast Loyalty
- **Special Ability:** Where You Go I Will Go — Ruth links herself to the most vulnerable allied unit on the field; both become completely invulnerable for 30 seconds, move twice as fast, and fight at double effectiveness; golden bond-lines of light connect them as a visual symbol of unbreakable covenant loyalty.
- **Passive Bonus:** Harvest Blessing — All food and grain resources gathered by the kingdom are increased by 30% while Ruth is active, inspired by her faithful labor in the harvest fields of Boaz that ultimately fed and saved Naomi.
- **Unlock Cost:** 700 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** Monastery Level 2
