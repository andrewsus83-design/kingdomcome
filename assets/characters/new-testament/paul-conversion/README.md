# PAUL (ROAD TO DAMASCUS)
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Acts 9, Acts 22, Acts 26, Galatians 1, Philippians 3
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Missionary, Author of 13 New Testament Letters
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Before he became the greatest missionary in history, Paul (then called Saul) was actually hunting down Christians to arrest them — until the day Jesus appeared to him in a blinding flash of light on the road to Damascus and asked, "Why are you persecuting me?" That one moment changed Paul completely and forever. He was blind for three days, and when his sight was restored, he dedicated his entire life to spreading the Gospel across the Roman Empire, writing 13 books of the New Testament and planting dozens of churches from Jerusalem to Rome.
