# JUDE THADDAEUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, John 14, Acts 1, Letter of Jude
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Patron of Hopeless Causes
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Jude Thaddaeus was one of Jesus's twelve apostles who asked Jesus a very thoughtful question at the Last Supper: "Lord, why do you show yourself to us and not to the whole world?" He became famous in the Church as the patron saint of hopeless and desperate causes — the saint to pray to when things seem impossible. He preached the Gospel in Mesopotamia, Persia, and Armenia, never giving up no matter how hard the mission became.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Hope in the Impossible
- **Special Ability:** Patron of Lost Causes — Jude's legendary intercession means that any failed or abandoned kingdom project can be revived at 50% of its original cost. Once per day, Jude can "rescue" one expired timer or failed mission and give it a second chance. Grants +25% to all prayer buildings.
