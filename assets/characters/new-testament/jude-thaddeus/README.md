# JUDE THADDEUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, John 14, Acts 1, Jude 1
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27 AD onward
**Role:** Patron of Hopeless Cases, Apostle
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Jude Thaddeus was one of Jesus's twelve apostles who asked a wonderfully thoughtful question at the Last Supper — "Lord, why do you show yourself to us and not to the whole world?" — showing he was always thinking deeply about how to bring more people to God. At Pentecost, when the Holy Spirit came like tongues of fire upon the disciples, Jude received that fire and it transformed him into a fearless missionary who traveled to Mesopotamia, Persia, and Armenia spreading the Good News. He became famous across the centuries as the Patron Saint of Hopeless and Desperate Causes — the saint people pray to when everything seems impossible and all other options have run out — because Jude believed that with God, absolutely nothing is ever truly hopeless.
