# SIMON THE ZEALOT
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, Acts 1
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Missionary
**Rarity:** Common

## Description
Simon the Zealot was one of Jesus's twelve apostles who got his nickname from the "Zealot" movement — a group of passionate Jewish people who fought hard for their nation's freedom. But Jesus transformed Simon's fiery passion from political fighting into spreading the love of God. Simon became one of the bravest missionaries of the early Church, carrying the Gospel to Persia and even to Egypt and Africa, showing that passionate people who follow Jesus can change the world.
