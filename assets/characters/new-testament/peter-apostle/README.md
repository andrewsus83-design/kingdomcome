# PETER THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4 & 16, Mark 1, Luke 5, John 1 & 21, Acts 1–15, 1–2 Peter
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Chief Apostle, First Pope, Rock of the Church
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Peter was a rugged fisherman named Simon when Jesus looked at him and said, "You are Peter, and on this rock I will build my Church." Jesus gave Peter the Keys of the Kingdom of Heaven and made him the leader of all the apostles — making Peter the first Pope. Peter was bold and sometimes made mistakes (he even denied knowing Jesus three times on the night of His arrest), but Jesus forgave him completely and gave him an even greater mission: "Feed my sheep." Peter led the early Church with unstoppable courage and became the first Bishop of Rome.
