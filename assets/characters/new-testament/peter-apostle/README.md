# PETER THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4 & 16, Mark 1, Luke 5, John 1 & 21, Acts 1–15, 1–2 Peter
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27–67 AD
**Role:** Chief Apostle, First Pope, Rock of the Church
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Peter was a rough and powerful fisherman named Simon when Jesus looked straight at him for the very first time and said, "You are Peter — and on this rock I will build my Church," handing him the Keys of the Kingdom of Heaven and making him the leader of all the apostles and the very first Pope. Peter was a man of enormous passion who could do extraordinary things — he stepped out of a boat and walked on water toward Jesus, he pulled out a sword at the arrest of Jesus to fight for Him, and he preached so powerfully at Pentecost that three thousand people joined the Church in a single day — but he was also a man who made real mistakes, including his most painful failure of all, denying three times that he even knew Jesus. But Jesus completely forgave Peter and gave him an even greater mission: "Feed my sheep" — asking him three times, once for each denial, to renew his love and lead the Church, making Peter's story one of the most powerful stories of redemption in the entire Bible.
