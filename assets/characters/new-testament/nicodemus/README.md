# NICODEMUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** John 3, John 7, John 19
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 30–33 AD
**Role:** Pharisee Who Came by Night, Seeker of Truth
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Nicodemus was a prominent Pharisee, a member of the Jewish ruling council, and one of the most respected teachers in Jerusalem — and he came to visit Jesus secretly at night because he was genuinely curious and open to learning, but afraid of what his important colleagues would think if they knew. During their extraordinary conversation, Jesus told Nicodemus the most famous sentence in the entire Bible: "For God so loved the world that He gave His only Son, so that everyone who believes in Him may not perish but may have eternal life" — John 3:16. Nicodemus appears two more times in the Gospel of John: once defending Jesus in front of the Pharisees with growing courage, and finally at the Cross, openly bringing expensive spices to help give Jesus a proper and honorable burial — showing that his secret faith had quietly grown into an act of public love.
