# NICODEMUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** John 3, John 7, John 19
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Pharisee, Secret Disciple, Seeker of Truth
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Nicodemus was an important Pharisee and teacher in Jerusalem who came to visit Jesus secretly at night because he was curious and open to learning but afraid of what others would think. During their conversation, Jesus taught him the famous words: "For God so loved the world that He gave His only Son, so that everyone who believes in Him may have eternal life" — one of the most important sentences in the entire Bible. Nicodemus grew in courage over time, and after Jesus died, he helped give Jesus a proper and honorable burial.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Seeking Truth with Courage
- **Special Ability:** Born Again — Nicodemus's nighttime conversation with Jesus unlocks a "Second Beginning" power that allows the player to reset any one building's upgrade tree and restart it from the top with a +15% permanent bonus applied. Also grants +30% to all education and teaching buildings while active.
