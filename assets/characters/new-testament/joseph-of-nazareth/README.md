# JOSEPH OF NAZARETH
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 1–2, Luke 1–2
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Foster Father of Jesus, Husband of Mary, Patron of Workers
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joseph of Nazareth was a carpenter in the small town of Nazareth who was chosen by God for one of the most important jobs in all of history: to be the earthly foster father of Jesus, the Son of God. Joseph was a just and gentle man who trusted God completely, even when things were confusing or difficult. He protected Mary and baby Jesus when King Herod was searching for them, leading the Holy Family safely to Egypt and back home again. He taught Jesus the carpenter's trade with patient loving hands, and the Church calls him the Patron of Workers, Fathers, and the Universal Church.
