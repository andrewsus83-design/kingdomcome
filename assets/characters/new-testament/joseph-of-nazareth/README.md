# JOSEPH OF NAZARETH
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 1–2, Luke 1–2
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 5 BC–30 AD
**Role:** Foster Father of Jesus, Patron of Workers and the Universal Church
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
Joseph of Nazareth was a carpenter in the small town of Nazareth who was chosen by God for one of the most important and demanding jobs in all of human history: to be the earthly father, protector, and provider for Jesus, the Son of God. Joseph was a just and completely trustworthy man who obeyed God even when obedience was terrifying — when an angel appeared to him in a dream and told him to take Mary as his wife and raise her son as his own, Joseph got up the very next morning and did exactly that without a word of complaint. When King Herod threatened the life of the infant Jesus, it was Joseph who received the urgent warning in a dream in the middle of the night, woke the family immediately, and led the Holy Family safely through the desert to Egypt and back again. He taught Jesus everything He knew about carpentry — the smell of fresh wood, the patience of precise work, the satisfaction of making something well with your hands — and the Church honors him as the Patron of Workers, the Patron of Fathers, and the Patron of the Universal Church.
