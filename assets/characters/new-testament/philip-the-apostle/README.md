# PHILIP THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, John 1 & 6 & 12 & 14, Acts 1 & 8
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27 AD onward
**Role:** Evangelist Apostle, the Practical Questioner
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Philip was one of the very first disciples Jesus called by name, and the first thing he ever did after meeting Jesus was run to find his friend Nathanael and announce with infectious excitement, "We have found the one Moses wrote about in the Law — Jesus of Nazareth, son of Joseph!" When Nathanael was skeptical, Philip's answer was brilliantly simple and eternally right: "Come and see!" Philip is famous for a remarkable moment at the feeding of the five thousand, when Jesus turned to Philip specifically and asked where they could buy enough bread for the crowd — and Philip calculated the logistics seriously and concluded it would take more than half a year's wages for everyone to get just a little bite, not yet realizing that Jesus was about to perform one of the greatest miracles in history. Philip also asked Jesus, "Lord, show us the Father," prompting Jesus to give one of His most profound answers: "Philip, anyone who has seen me has seen the Father."
