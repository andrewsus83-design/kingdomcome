# PHILIP THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, John 1, John 6, John 12, John 14, Acts 8
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Deacon, Evangelist
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Philip was one of the first disciples Jesus called by name, and he immediately went to find his friend Nathanael to share the exciting news. Philip plays a key role in the miracle of the feeding of the 5,000 — Jesus asked Philip where they could buy enough bread for the crowd, testing his faith. As a deacon in the early Church, Philip baptized the Ethiopian official on the road to Gaza, spreading the Gospel all the way to Africa, making him one of the great early missionaries.
