# LAZARUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** John 11, John 12
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 30 AD
**Role:** Friend of Jesus, Man Raised from the Dead
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Lazarus was one of Jesus's closest and most beloved friends, living in Bethany with his sisters Mary and Martha, and the Bible tells us simply and tenderly that "Jesus loved Lazarus." When Lazarus became seriously ill and died, Jesus wept at his tomb — and those two words, "Jesus wept," are the shortest verse in the entire Bible and one of the most powerful, showing us that the Son of God felt the full weight of human grief. Then Jesus did something no one had ever done before: He stood before the sealed tomb, cried out with a voice of complete divine authority — "Lazarus, come forth!" — and the dead man walked out, still wrapped in his burial cloths, alive and blinking in the sunlight. The crowd was so overwhelmed that many of them believed in Jesus right then and there.
