# LAZARUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** John 11, John 12
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Friend of Jesus, Miracle of Resurrection
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Lazarus was one of Jesus's closest and dearest friends, along with his sisters Mary and Martha in Bethany. When Lazarus became very sick and died, Jesus wept at his tomb — the shortest and most tender verse in the Bible — and then performed one of His greatest miracles: calling Lazarus back to life after four days in the tomb. Lazarus walked out still wrapped in his burial cloths, and the whole crowd was amazed. This miracle was so powerful that many people believed in Jesus because of it.
