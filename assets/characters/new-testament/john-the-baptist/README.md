# JOHN THE BAPTIST
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 3, Mark 1, Luke 1 & 3, John 1
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Prophet, Forerunner of Christ
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
John the Baptist was the last great prophet of the Old Testament era, sent by God to prepare the world for the coming of Jesus. He lived in the wilderness, eating locusts and wild honey, and called people to turn away from sin and be baptized in the Jordan River. When Jesus came to him, John recognized Him immediately and declared, "Behold the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!"
