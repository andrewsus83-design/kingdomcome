# JOHN THE BAPTIST
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 3 & 11, Mark 1 & 6, Luke 1 & 3, John 1 & 3
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27–28 AD
**Role:** Prophet and Forerunner of Christ
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
John the Baptist was the greatest prophet ever born, chosen by God before he was even born to prepare the entire world for the coming of Jesus — and his miraculous story began when his mother Elizabeth and Jesus's mother Mary, both pregnant at the same time, met together and the baby John leaped for joy in the womb! John grew up in the desert wilderness eating locusts and wild honey, wearing a rough camel-hair cloak, and he thundered God's call to repentance to enormous crowds at the Jordan River. When Jesus finally came to be baptized, John recognized Him at once and made the most important announcement in history: "Behold the Lamb of God, who takes away the sin of the world!"
