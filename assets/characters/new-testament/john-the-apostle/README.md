# JOHN THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Gospel of John, 1–3 John, Revelation
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Evangelist, Visionary
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
John the Apostle was the youngest of Jesus's twelve apostles and known as "the disciple whom Jesus loved." He stood at the foot of the cross with Mary, and Jesus trusted him so much that He asked John to care for His mother. John wrote one of the four Gospels, three letters of the New Testament, and the Book of Revelation — more New Testament books than almost anyone else. He is the only apostle believed to have died peacefully of old age on the island of Patmos.
