# JOHN THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4 & 17, Mark 1 & 9, Luke 5 & 9, John 1–21, Acts 3–4, Galatians 2, 1–3 John, Revelation 1–22
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27–100 AD
**Role:** Beloved Apostle, Eagle of the Evangelists, Author of Revelation
**Rarity:** Legendary

## Description
John the Apostle was the youngest of all twelve apostles and also the closest to Jesus's heart — at the Last Supper he leaned right against Jesus, and at the Cross he was the only male apostle brave enough to stay and watch, which is why Jesus looked down from the Cross and entrusted His own mother Mary into John's personal care. John went on to write five books of the Bible — the Gospel of John, three letters, and the breathtaking Book of Revelation — which he wrote while exiled on the rocky island of Patmos where God showed him extraordinary visions of heaven, angels, and the end of time. He is called the "Eagle of the Evangelists" because his Gospel soars the highest into the mystery of who Jesus truly is, opening with the most majestic sentence ever written: "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
