# STEPHEN THE MARTYR
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Acts 6–7
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 33–36 AD
**Role:** First Christian Martyr, Protomartyr, First Deacon
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Stephen was one of the very first seven deacons chosen by the apostles to serve the early Church, and the Bible describes him as a man "full of grace and power" who performed extraordinary signs and wonders — and so filled with wisdom that not a single person could successfully argue against him when he spoke about Jesus. Stephen preached the most brilliant and fearless sermon in the entire Book of Acts, walking through the entire history of Israel to show that Jesus was the fulfillment of everything God had promised — and when the crowd turned against him, he looked up and saw heaven opening and Jesus Himself standing at the right hand of God. He died as the very first Christian martyr, and with his last breath he forgave those who were hurting him, just as Jesus had forgiven from the Cross — making him a mirror image of Christ in his final moments.
