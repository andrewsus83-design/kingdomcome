# STEPHEN THE MARTYR
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Acts 6–7
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Deacon, First Christian Martyr, Protomartyr
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Stephen was one of the first seven deacons chosen by the apostles to help serve the early Church, and the Bible says he was "full of grace and power" and performed great miracles. He was so wise and Spirit-filled that no one could argue against him when he spoke about Jesus. Stephen became the very first Christian martyr — the first person to give his life for his faith in Jesus — and as he died, he saw a vision of heaven opening and Jesus standing at God's right hand, and he forgave those who were hurting him, just like Jesus on the cross.
