# JAMES THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4 & 17, Mark 1 & 3 & 9, Luke 5 & 9, Acts 1 & 12
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27–44 AD
**Role:** Son of Thunder, First Apostle Martyr
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
James the Apostle was the older brother of John and together they were so bold and passionate that Jesus nicknamed them the "Sons of Thunder" — which tells you everything about James's fiery, fearless personality. He was one of only three apostles (along with Peter and John) privileged to witness the Transfiguration, where Jesus's face shone like the sun on Mount Tabor and His clothes blazed dazzling white — an experience so overwhelming that James fell face-down to the ground trembling. James became the first of the twelve apostles to die for his faith in Jesus, executed by King Herod Agrippa around 44 AD, making him the bold and glorious first martyr among the Twelve — and today he is one of the most celebrated saints in the world, with hundreds of thousands of pilgrims walking the famous Camino de Santiago in Spain to honor him every single year.
