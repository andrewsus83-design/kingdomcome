# JAMES THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4 & 17, Mark 1 & 9, Luke 5 & 9, Acts 12
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, First Apostle Martyr
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
James was one of Jesus's closest apostles — part of the inner circle with Peter and John — and was one of the first to be called away from his fishing nets to follow Jesus. He was present at the Transfiguration when Jesus appeared in dazzling glory on the mountaintop. James was the first of the twelve apostles to give his life for his faith in Jesus, making him a great hero of courage. He is the patron saint of pilgrims, and pilgrims walk hundreds of miles to his famous shrine in Santiago de Compostela, Spain.
