# MATTHEW THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Gospel of Matthew, Matthew 9–10
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Evangelist, Author of the First Gospel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Matthew was a tax collector — one of the most disliked jobs in ancient Israel — until the day Jesus walked past his tax booth and simply said, "Follow me." Matthew immediately left everything, threw a great feast to celebrate, and became one of the twelve apostles. He wrote the Gospel of Matthew, the very first book of the New Testament, which begins with the genealogy of Jesus and was written especially to show Jewish people that Jesus was the promised Messiah.
