# MATTHEW THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 9–10, Mark 2, Luke 5, Acts 1
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27 AD onward
**Role:** Tax Collector Turned Evangelist, Author of the First Gospel
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Matthew was sitting at his tax collection booth one ordinary morning in Capernaum — a man whom most people despised because tax collectors worked for the Roman occupiers and often cheated people of their hard-earned money — when Jesus walked up, looked straight at him, and simply said "Follow me." Matthew stood up immediately, left everything behind, and threw the most spectacular dinner party in Capernaum to celebrate his new life, inviting all his fellow tax collectors to meet Jesus, which scandalized the religious leaders who couldn't believe Jesus would eat with such people. Matthew went on to write the most Jewish of all four Gospels — carefully showing how Jesus fulfilled every single Old Testament prophecy — and because Matthew's Gospel opens the entire New Testament, his words are the very first thing you read when you open the New Testament, making him the literary gateway to the whole story of salvation.
