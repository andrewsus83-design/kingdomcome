# BARTHOLOMEW (NATHANAEL)
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, John 1 & 21, Acts 1
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27 AD onward
**Role:** Apostle Under the Fig Tree, Man of No Guile
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Bartholomew — also known as Nathanael — has one of the most surprising first meetings with Jesus in all the Gospels: when Philip told him about Jesus of Nazareth, Nathanael said with skeptical honesty, "Can anything good come from Nazareth?" But then Jesus saw him coming from a distance and made a statement that stopped Nathanael cold: "Here is a true Israelite in whom there is no guile." When Nathanael asked how Jesus knew him, Jesus revealed that He had seen Nathanael sitting under the fig tree before Philip ever called him — which was impossible unless Jesus was truly divine — and Nathanael was so overwhelmed that he immediately declared, "Rabbi, you are the Son of God; you are the King of Israel!" He went on to become a devoted apostle who carried the Gospel all the way to Armenia and India, and is venerated to this day as a great saint and martyr of extraordinary sincerity.
