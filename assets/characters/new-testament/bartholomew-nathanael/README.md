# BARTHOLOMEW (NATHANAEL)
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, John 1, Acts 1
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Missionary
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Bartholomew, also known as Nathanael, was an apostle of Jesus who was sitting under a fig tree in quiet prayer when Jesus saw him from far away and declared, "Here is a true Israelite, in whom there is nothing false!" Nathanael was shocked that Jesus knew him before they had even met, and this miraculous knowledge convinced him immediately to follow Jesus. He is known for his pure heart and transparent honesty, and he carried the Gospel as far as Armenia and India.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Purity of Heart
- **Special Ability:** Nothing Hidden — Nathanael's transparent honesty means that all hidden costs, timers, and requirements in the kingdom are permanently revealed on the interface while he is active. Additionally grants +20% to all prayer and chapel buildings, and has a 15% chance to double the reward from any daily mission.
- **Unlock Cost:** 600 HolyPoints
