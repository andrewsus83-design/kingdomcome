# ANDREW THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4 & 10, Mark 1 & 13, Luke 6, John 1 & 6 & 12
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27–60 AD
**Role:** First Called Apostle, Patron of Scotland and Russia
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Andrew holds the extraordinary honor of being the very first person Jesus ever called to follow Him — and the moment Jesus said "Come, follow me," Andrew dropped his fishing net immediately and never looked back, then ran straight to find his brother Simon Peter and bring him to Jesus too, making Andrew the very first Christian evangelist in history. Andrew was also the apostle who, when five thousand hungry people needed feeding, bravely brought forward a young boy who had five loaves of bread and two fish — that small act of practical faith triggered one of Jesus's greatest miracles. He spent the rest of his life traveling thousands of miles to bring the Good News to Greece, Asia Minor, and beyond, and when he was finally arrested and sentenced to crucifixion, he is said to have greeted his X-shaped cross with joy, saying he was not worthy to die on the same shape of cross as his Lord.
