# ANDREW THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 4, Mark 1, Luke 5, John 1 & 6
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Fisherman, Missionary
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Andrew was a fisherman on the Sea of Galilee who became the very first person Jesus called to follow Him — even before his brother Peter! He immediately ran to find Peter and told him, "We have found the Messiah!" Andrew was always the one bringing people to Jesus, including the boy with five loaves and two fish before the miracle feeding of 5,000 people. He went on to preach the Gospel in Greece and Russia, and is the patron saint of Scotland, Russia, and Greece.
