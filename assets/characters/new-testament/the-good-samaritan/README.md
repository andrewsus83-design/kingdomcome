# THE GOOD SAMARITAN
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Luke 10:25–37
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Parable Character, Model of Neighbor-Love
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
The Good Samaritan is the hero of one of Jesus's most famous parables, told to answer the question "Who is my neighbor?" A man was beaten and left hurt on the road to Jericho, and several religious leaders passed by without helping — but a Samaritan (someone from a group that was looked down upon) stopped, bandaged the man's wounds, put him on his donkey, brought him to an inn, and paid for his care. Jesus told this story to teach us that loving our neighbor means helping anyone who needs us, no matter who they are.
