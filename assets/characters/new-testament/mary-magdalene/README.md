# MARY MAGDALENE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 27–28, Mark 15–16, Luke 8 & 23–24, John 19–20
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 28–30 AD
**Role:** Apostle to the Apostles, First Witness to the Resurrection
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Mary Magdalene was one of Jesus's most faithful and courageous disciples — a woman from the town of Magdala whom Jesus healed completely, and who followed Him all the way to the Cross when many others had run away in fear. After Jesus died and was buried, it was Mary Magdalene who rose before dawn on Easter Sunday, went to the tomb first of all, and had the most extraordinary surprise in the history of the world: the stone was rolled away and the tomb was empty — and then the Risen Jesus Himself appeared to her in the garden and called her by name. Jesus chose Mary Magdalene to be the very first person to announce His Resurrection to the other disciples, which is why she is called the "Apostle to the Apostles" — one of the greatest honors in the entire Bible.
