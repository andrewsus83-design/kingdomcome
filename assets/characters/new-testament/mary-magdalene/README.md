# MARY MAGDALENE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 27–28, Mark 15–16, Luke 8 & 24, John 19–20
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Disciple, First Witness of the Resurrection
**Rarity:** Epic

## Description
Mary Magdalene was one of Jesus's most devoted followers, who traveled with Him and supported His ministry. She stood faithfully at the foot of the cross when Jesus died, and on Easter morning she was the very first person Jesus appeared to after rising from the dead. Because of this, she is called the "Apostle to the Apostles" — Jesus sent her to tell the other disciples the greatest news in history: He is risen!
