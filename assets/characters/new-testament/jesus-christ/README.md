# JESUS CHRIST
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Revelation (entire New Testament)
**Era:** 1st Century AD — Incarnation, Ministry, Passion, Resurrection, Ascension
**Role:** Son of God, Savior of the World, King of Kings
**Rarity:** Divine (Unique — above all rarity tiers)

## Description
Jesus Christ is the Son of God made man — fully divine and fully human — who came to Earth to save every single person who has ever lived. He performed miracles that no one else could do: healing the blind, raising the dead, calming storms with a single word, and feeding five thousand people with five loaves of bread and two fish. The most astounding thing Jesus ever did was die on the Cross out of pure love for us, then rise from the dead three days later — defeating sin and death forever — which is why He is the most important person in all of history and the blazing heart of this entire game.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Divine Love
- **Special Ability:** Divine Mercy — Jesus radiates His Sacred Heart in a blazing pulse of golden-rose light that instantly heals every building, character, and resource in the entire kingdom simultaneously. All resource production is doubled for 1 full hour, all wounded characters are fully restored, and every locked area in the kingdom is opened. A visible halo of warm white light expands outward from Him across the entire kingdom map during activation, with tiny golden sparks lingering for 60 seconds after.
- **Passive Bonus:** Presence of the Lord — while Jesus is placed in any zone of the kingdom, all characters in that zone receive +30% to all stats, prayers complete 2x faster, and no enemy or obstacle can enter that zone.
- **Unlock Cost:** 0 HolyPoints — gifted to every player at the start of the game
- **Required Building:** None (available immediately; upgrades with The Temple, Level 5)
