# JESUS CHRIST
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew, Mark, Luke, John, Revelation
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Son of God, Savior of the World
**Rarity:** Divine (Unique Tier — above all rarity classes)

## Description
Jesus Christ is the Son of God, the Second Person of the Holy Trinity, who came to earth as a human being to save all people from sin and death. He performed miracles, taught the world to love God and neighbor, and gave His life on the cross — rising again on Easter Sunday in the greatest victory in history. He is the center of the entire Kingdom Come universe and the most powerful, radiant, and loving character in the game.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Divine Love (Agape)
- **Special Ability:** Divine Mercy — Heals all kingdom damage and doubles all resources for 1 hour. When activated, a Sacred Heart pulse radiates from the player's kingdom center, restoring all damaged buildings to full health and blessing every active character with a golden glow.
- **Unlock Cost:** 0 HolyPoints (Jesus is gifted to every player at the start of the game)
- **Required Building:** The Chapel (Level 1) — Jesus unlocks at the very beginning of the journey
