# THOMAS THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Matthew 10, Mark 3, Luke 6, John 11 & 14 & 20–21
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 27–72 AD
**Role:** The Doubter Who Became the Believer, Patron of India
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Thomas the Apostle has been called "Doubting Thomas" for two thousand years, but his story is really one of the most courageous and beautiful in the whole Bible — when the other disciples told him they had seen the Risen Jesus, Thomas said honestly that he would need to see and touch the wounds himself before he could believe, and when Jesus appeared specifically for Thomas and invited him to touch His hands and side, Thomas immediately fell to his knees and made the greatest declaration of faith in the entire Gospel: "My Lord and my God!" — which is still one of the most powerful prayers a person can say. Jesus then said, "Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed," and Thomas proved his faith was total by traveling further than any other apostle — all the way to India — where he planted a church that still exists today, dying as a martyr for the Lord he had once doubted.
