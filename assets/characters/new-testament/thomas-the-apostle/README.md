# THOMAS THE APOSTLE
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** John 11, John 14, John 20, John 21
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Apostle, Missionary to India
**Rarity:** Rare

## Description
Thomas is often called "Doubting Thomas" because after the Resurrection he said he would not believe unless he could see and touch Jesus's wounds himself — but when Jesus appeared to him, Thomas made the most powerful declaration of faith in the whole Gospel: "My Lord and my God!" Far from staying a doubter, Thomas became one of the boldest missionaries, traveling all the way to India to share the Good News, where Christians still trace their faith back to him today.

## Game Stats
- **Virtue:** Faith Through Seeking
- **Special Ability:** My Lord and My God — Thomas's great act of faith unlocks a "Doubt to Belief" mechanic where any faith-deficit penalties in the kingdom are instantly cleared and converted into a +35% faith boost for 30 minutes. Also grants a one-time building inspection that reveals the true strength of any structure.
- **Unlock Cost:** 650 HolyPoints
- **Required Building:** The Upper Room (Level 2)
