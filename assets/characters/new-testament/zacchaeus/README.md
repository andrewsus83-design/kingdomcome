# ZACCHAEUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Luke 19:1–10
**Era:** 1st Century AD
**Role:** Tax Collector, Converted Disciple
**Rarity:** Common

## Description
Zacchaeus was a chief tax collector in Jericho who had become very rich but was not loved by his community because of his dishonest ways. When Jesus came through town, Zacchaeus was so eager to see Him that he climbed a sycamore tree to get a better look — even though he was a grown adult! Jesus surprised everyone by looking up, calling Zacchaeus by name, and saying He would have dinner at his house. Zacchaeus immediately decided to give half his money to the poor and pay back four times anything he had taken dishonestly — showing how a single encounter with Jesus can change everything.
