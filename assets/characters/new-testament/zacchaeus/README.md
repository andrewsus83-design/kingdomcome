# ZACCHAEUS
**Testament:** New Testament
**Book(s):** Luke 19:1–10
**Era:** 1st Century AD — approximately 29–30 AD
**Role:** Chief Tax Collector Converted, Disciple of Jesus
**Rarity:** Common

## Description
Zacchaeus was the chief tax collector of Jericho — the most senior and therefore the most disliked tax man in the whole city — who had become very rich but was deeply lonely because no one trusted or respected him. When Jesus came through town, Zacchaeus was so desperately eager to see Him that he actually ran ahead and climbed a sycamore tree to get a better view, which was a hilarious thing for a wealthy important official to do. But Jesus stopped directly under that tree, looked straight up, and called him by his full name: "Zacchaeus, come down immediately — I must stay at your house today." Zacchaeus scrambled down with pure joy, and that very same day he announced he would give half his fortune to the poor and pay back four times anything he had taken dishonestly — showing the whole crowd that a single moment with Jesus can completely transform a person's heart.
